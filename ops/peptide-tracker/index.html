Your doses — what each one is doing
Retatrutide weekly · Mon AM
GLP-1 / GIP / glucagon triple agonist — your main fat-loss driver.
  • Job: cuts appetite, raises energy burn + liver-fat oxidation.
  • Dose note: 4 mg (40u). If losing >2 lb/wk near goal, ease to 3 mg (30u) to protect muscle. Don't climb past 40u for this goal.
  • Timeline: appetite drop in days; steady loss, no plateau yet.
  • watch nausea/GI (worst right after dose), constipation, resting HR +5–8 bpm.
  • muscle ~35% of weight lost can be lean mass → protein + lifting are non-negotiable.
  • serious pancreatitis (severe belly pain → back), gallbladder, rapid-loss gallstones.
Tesamorelin nightly · bedtime fasted
GHRH analog — raises your own GH/IGF-1; targets visceral fat + recovery.
  • Timing: bedtime, no food 2h before (insulin blunts the GH pulse). Sleep is where it works.
  • Dose note: 1.5 mg now (stepping 1→2 mg). Get an IGF-1 lab before going to 20u/2 mg.
  • watch joint aches, puffiness/water retention, wrist tingling (carpal tunnel) = signs to ease down.
  • sugar GH mildly raises blood glucose — relevant alongside Reta.
  • Rule: never push IGF-1 above your age range.
Semax daily · AM only
Nootropic peptide (BDNF/focus). Lowest-risk, lowest-evidence of the three.
  • Timing: morning, last dose by ~3–4 PM or it hurts sleep.
  • Dose note: 650 mcg; 300–600 mcg is the studied range — could drop to ~3u and stretch the vial.
  • watch mild overstimulation, headache, insomnia if late. Additive with caffeine/cocaine.

Risks — weed + cocaine on this stack
🚬 Cocaine serious
  • The heart is the issue. Reta keeps resting HR up 24/7 (6-day half-life — no "off week"). Coke spikes HR/BP + constricts arteries → real heart-attack/stroke risk, stacked.
  • Coke + alcohol = cocaethylene — more cardiotoxic, lasts longer. Biggest party-night danger since you'll be drinking.
  • Dehydration (Reta + alcohol + coke) strains kidneys → hydrate + electrolytes hard.
  • Pancreatitis risk is additive with the GLP-1.
  • Appetite: kills it on top of Reta → undereating + muscle loss.
  • Keep it genuinely occasional. Chest pain/pressure or a heart that won't settle = ER, not sleep-it-off.
🌿 Weed blunts results
  • Munchies fight the cut — aim them at protein, not junk.
  • Don't eat in the 2h before Tesa — a late snack wastes the GH pulse. The #1 weed habit that costs you.
  • Chronic use degrades deep sleep = exactly where Tesa works.
  • upside anti-nausea (eases Reta GI); HR bump is small vs coke.
🚨 Red flags — stop & see a doctor
  • Severe belly pain radiating to the back (± vomiting) → pancreatitis
  • Right-upper belly pain after fatty food, fever, jaundice → gallbladder
  • Persistent vomiting / dehydration
  • Racing or pounding heart, palpitations
  • Vision changes · neck lump/hoarseness · rash/swelling/trouble breathing
  • Tesa: bad swelling, joint pain, wrist tingling = dosing too high
🩸 Labs to monitor
IGF-1 (gate for Tesa 2 mg) · fasting glucose + HbA1c · lipase · lipids · CMP (kidney/liver) · TSH/free T4 · CBC · resting HR + BP.
