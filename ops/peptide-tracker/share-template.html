General safety reference
🚨 Red flags — stop & see a doctor
  • Severe belly pain radiating to the back (± vomiting) → pancreatitis
  • Right-upper belly pain after fatty food, fever, jaundice → gallbladder
  • Persistent vomiting / dehydration
  • Racing or pounding heart, palpitations
  • Vision changes · neck lump/hoarseness · rash/swelling/trouble breathing
  • GH peptides: bad swelling, joint pain, wrist tingling = dosing too high
🩸 Labs worth monitoring
IGF-1 (for GH peptides — keep in age range) · fasting glucose + HbA1c · lipase · lipids · CMP (kidney/liver) · TSH/free T4 · CBC · resting HR + BP.
Schedule: baseline → 3 months → 6–12 months.
⚠️ Substance interactions (general)
  • Stimulants (cocaine, amphetamines): GLP-1 drugs raise resting HR; stimulants spike HR/BP → additive cardiac strain. Cocaine + alcohol forms cocaethylene (more cardiotoxic).
  • Alcohol: dehydration + GI upset + adds pancreatitis risk on GLP-1 drugs.
  • Cannabis: munchies fight appetite suppression; eating before a bedtime GH-peptide dose blunts the GH pulse; chronic use degrades deep sleep.
  • Hydrate + electrolytes, keep stimulant use minimal, and don't eat in the 2h before a bedtime GH peptide.
Educational / harm-reduction only — not medical advice.
