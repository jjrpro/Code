# 🩸 BASELINE LAB CHECKLIST — JR (peptide stack monitoring)

**Date:** 2026-05-31
**Summary:** Printable list of labs to draw before/while running the Reta + Tesa + Semax stack.
Hand this to a clinic, or order yourself through a direct-to-consumer lab. Get the **baseline
NOW** (especially IGF-1 — it gatekeeps taking Tesamorelin up to 2 mg).

> Tip: most of these come bundled. Ask for a **"comprehensive metabolic/wellness panel + IGF-1
> + lipase + HbA1c + lipids + thyroid + CBC."** A direct-to-consumer service (e.g. an online
> lab that uses Quest/LabCorp draw sites) can order all of it without a doctor visit in most US states.

---

## ✅ THE PANEL (copy/paste to a lab or clinic)

| Test | Why it's on the list | Flag if… |
|---|---|---|
| **IGF-1** | Tesamorelin raises it — **the #1 lab.** Don't push above age range. | Above age/sex normal range → don't raise Tesa / back off |
| **HbA1c** | 3-month blood-sugar average (GH ↑, Reta ↓) | Rising toward 5.7%+ |
| **Fasting glucose** | Same — net glucose effect | >100 mg/dL fasting |
| **Lipid panel** (LDL/HDL/trig) | Both drugs shift lipids | LDL/trig climbing |
| **Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP)** | Kidney (creatinine/eGFR) + liver (ALT/AST) + electrolytes | Any out-of-range |
| **Lipase** (± amylase) | Pancreatitis screen for Reta | >3× upper limit **with** belly pain = urgent |
| **TSH + Free T4** | GH can lower Free T4 | Free T4 low / TSH off |
| **CBC** | General safety baseline | Any flag |
| **Testosterone (total + free)** | Big fat loss + training — useful baseline for muscle goals | Low → discuss with doctor |

**Vitals to log at home (free, no lab):**
- **Resting heart rate** (Reta raises it ~5–8 bpm) — check most mornings
- **Blood pressure** — periodically
- **Waist measurement** at navel — every 1–2 weeks

---

## 📅 WHEN TO DRAW

| Timing | What |
|---|---|
| **NOW (baseline)** | Full panel above — *before* taking Tesa to 2 mg |
| **~3 months** | Repeat IGF-1, HbA1c, glucose, lipase, CMP |
| **~6–12 months** | Full panel again |
| **Anytime** | If a red-flag symptom shows up (see cheat sheet) |

---

## 🧪 PREP FOR THE DRAW
- **Fast 8–12 h** (water only) — needed for accurate glucose + lipids. Morning draw is easiest.
- **Take Semax as normal**; the morning lab won't be affected.
- For the cleanest IGF-1 reading, draw it on a **normal dosing stretch** (not right after a missed week).

---

## 💡 HOW TO ORDER WITHOUT A DOCTOR (US)
1. Online direct-to-consumer lab services let you buy the panel and get a requisition emailed.
2. You walk into a **Quest or LabCorp** patient site for the blood draw.
3. Results come back online in a few days.
4. Search the bundle as: *"men's hormone + metabolic panel with IGF-1, lipase, HbA1c."*
5. If anything flags, **bring the results to a doctor** — don't self-interpret borderline values.
