## 🎯 PHASED PLAN (260→200 done · →185 · then recomp)
**Rule: Reta goes DOWN to build muscle. You can't max-cut + max-build at once.**

| | **Phase 1: cut to 185** | **Phase 2: recomp @185** |
|---|---|---|
| **Reta** | hold **4 mg (40u)/wk** — don't climb | taper **~2 mg (20u)/wk** or q10–14d |
| **Tesa** | **1.5–2 mg (15–20u)** *after IGF-1* | hold **2 mg (20u)**, IGF-1 in range |
| **Semax** | **300–600 mcg (3–6u) AM** | same |
| **Eat** | deficit, protein 185–200g, ~1–2 lb/wk | **maintenance**, protein 185–200g |

Stall at 185 ≠ more Reta. **Track waist + strength, not just scale.**

## 📅 DAILY RHYTHM
- **Wake:** Semax → biggest-protein meal → water + electrolytes
- **Midday:** protein meal + **lift** (3–4×/wk)
- **Afternoon:** protein snack · *last Semax by 3–4 PM*
- **Dinner:** protein-first, earlier, low-carb · finish ≥2h before bed
- **Bed:** Tesamorelin (fasted) → **7–9h sleep**
- **Weekly:** Retatrutide on your fixed day (Sun?) — expect GI 24–48h after

## 💪 WIN/LOSE LEVERS
- **Protein 1.6–2.2 g/kg/day** — eat protein FIRST, on a schedule (not hunger), front-loaded AM
- **Lift 3–4×/week** — this is what keeps muscle while fat drops
- **Electrolytes + water** daily — kills the "GLP-1 fatigue"
- **Don't chase fast loss** — gallstone risk; ~0.5–1 kg/wk is the safe pace
- **Body-comp scan every 8–12 wks** — confirm it's fat, not muscle

## 🚨 STOP & CALL A DOCTOR
- Severe belly pain → back (± vomiting) = **pancreatitis**
- Right-upper belly pain after fatty food, fever, yellow skin = **gallbladder**
- Won't-stop vomiting / dehydration
- Racing/pounding heart, palpitations
- Shakes/confusion/sweats = **low blood sugar**
- Vision changes
- Neck lump, hoarseness, trouble swallowing = **thyroid**
- Rash/swelling/trouble breathing = **allergy**
- Tesa: bad swelling, joint pain, wrist tingling = **dosing too high**

## 🔧 MIXING & SHOTS
Swab both stoppers · run water **down the glass wall** · **swirl, don't shake** ·
fridge 2–8°C, dark, **never freeze** · toss if cloudy/floaters/color change ·
**new needle every time** · rotate sites (abdomen, avoid navel) · never share · sharps bin
