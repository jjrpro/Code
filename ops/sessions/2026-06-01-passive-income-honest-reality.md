## The honest part (read this first)

A business that needs **$0 from you, 0 effort from you, and runs 100% on me** —
does not exist. Not because I'm lazy or you're missing a trick. Because of three
hard limits that no clever setup gets around:

1. **Money has to land in an account tied to a real human — you.**
   Every payout rail (Stripe, PayPal, AdSense, a bank, a crypto exchange,
   Coinbase) legally requires identity verification (KYC). I can't be that
   human. I can't pass ID checks, sign terms, or receive funds. So *someone's*
   identity — yours — has to anchor the money. That's a one-time setup, but it's
   non-zero effort and it's unavoidable.

2. **I'm not always on.** I run in sessions, not 24/7. "Always-on autopilot"
   requires a tiny host (e.g. Render) that I set up and maintain, but that
   *exists* between my sessions. Free tiers exist, but reliable ones cost a few
   dollars a month.

3. **Distribution is the actual hard part — and it's never free.**
   Making the *product* (videos, content packs, a store) is the easy 10%. Getting
   eyeballs/buyers is the 90%, and it costs either money (ads) or time (organic
   reach building over months). No tool removes this.

**Bottom line:** the real minimum isn't "$0 and nothing." It's **"about $40 once,
two 10-minute account setups by you, then I genuinely run the rest."** Anyone who
tells you less than that is selling you something.
