# Reta + Workout Plan — JR's Sister (190 → 165, 5'8")

**Date:** 2026-06-15
**Summary:** Beginner-safe Retatrutide dosing (in JR's units method) plus a full beginner→
intermediate workout + nutrition plan to lose ~25 lb while keeping/building muscle (toned, not skinny).

> Educational / harm-reduction only — not medical advice. A new user should ideally have a
> doctor + baseline labs. Rule out the contraindications below before the first dose.

---

## ⚠️ Before she starts (new female user)
- **Pregnancy = hard stop.** Not for anyone pregnant, trying, or possibly pregnant.
- **Birth control:** GLP-1 drugs can reduce pill absorption during dose increases → use a
  **backup method (condoms)** through the first ~8 weeks.
- **Don't start (without a doctor) if** personal/family history of **medullary thyroid cancer /
  MEN2**, or prior **pancreatitis**.
- **Start low, go slow** — she's new; lowest effective dose wins.

---

## Dosing — same units method

**Reconstitution (do it exactly like this so the math holds):**
**30 mg vial + 300 units (3 mL) BAC water = 0.1 mg per unit → 10 units = 1 mg.**
⚠️ Only valid with this exact mix. Different vial size or water volume = different mg/unit → recompute.

**Titration — once weekly, same day, subcutaneous, rotate sites:**

| Weeks | Dose | Units | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | 1 mg | **10u** | Gentle break-in, minimizes nausea |
| 5–8 | 2 mg | **20u** | Most people lose well here |
| 9–12 | 3 mg | **30u** | Only if loss stalls AND tolerating well |
| 13+ | 4 mg | **40u** | Likely the most she'd ever need for 165 |

**Rules:**
- Hold at the **lowest dose still producing ~1–1.5 lb/week.** Don't climb just to climb.
- Nausea? **Stay on the current dose longer** instead of increasing.
- Faster loss = more muscle loss + gallstone risk. Slow is better.

**Side effects / red flags:** nausea, constipation, resting HR bump (normal-ish, worst after
increases). **Severe belly pain radiating to the back = stop, possible pancreatitis.** Rapid loss
can cause gallstones — keep some dietary fat, stay hydrated.

**Labs (ideal):** baseline + ~3 mo: fasting glucose/HbA1c, lipase, lipids, CMP, thyroid; track
resting HR at home.

## Workout — 4-day plan (best results), with 3-day fallback

Goal: lose fat **and keep/build muscle** so she ends up toned, not soft. Resistance training is
the non-negotiable that protects muscle in a calorie deficit; cardio/steps handle extra burn.

**Progression rule (the whole game):** each session try to beat last time — 1 more rep or a bit
more weight. When she hits the top of the rep range on all sets, add weight and drop back to the
bottom. Log it in a notes app. Leave 1–2 reps "in the tank," don't grind to failure.
Warm up 5 min (walk/bike) + 1 light set of the first lift. Rest ~60–90s (small moves), 2 min (big lifts).

### 4-DAY: Lower / Upper / Lower / Upper (e.g. Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri)

**LOWER A (glute/quad focus)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|
| Goblet squat (or leg press) | 3 × 8–12 |
| Romanian deadlift (DB or barbell) | 3 × 8–12 |
| Hip thrust / glute bridge | 3 × 10–15 |
| Walking lunges | 2 × 10–12 per leg |
| Calf raise | 3 × 12–20 |
| Plank | 3 × 30–45s |

**UPPER A**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|
| DB chest press (or push-up) | 3 × 8–12 |
| Lat pulldown (or assisted pull-up) | 3 × 8–12 |
| Seated row | 3 × 10–12 |
| DB shoulder press | 3 × 10–12 |
| Biceps curl | 2 × 12–15 |
| Triceps pushdown | 2 × 12–15 |

**LOWER B (hamstring/glute focus)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|
| Hip thrust (heavier) | 3 × 8–12 |
| Leg press (or split squat) | 3 × 10–12 |
| Leg curl | 3 × 12–15 |
| Step-ups | 2 × 10 per leg |
| Cable kickback / abduction | 2 × 15 |
| Hanging knee raise / crunch | 3 × 12–15 |

**UPPER B**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|
| Incline DB press | 3 × 10–12 |
| Pull-up / pulldown (variation) | 3 × 8–12 |
| Chest-supported row | 3 × 10–12 |
| Lateral raise | 3 × 12–15 |
| Face pull | 2 × 15 |
| Curl + pushdown superset | 2 × 12–15 each |

### 3-DAY FALLBACK (busy weeks) — Full Body, Mon/Wed/Fri
Each day: 1 squat move · 1 hinge (RDL/hip thrust) · 1 push · 1 pull · 1 core. 3 sets, 8–12 reps,
progressive overload. Rotate exercises so it's not identical each day.

### Cardio / steps
- **8,000–10,000 steps/day** — the biggest, easiest fat-loss lever.
- Optional **2× 20–30 min** easy cardio (incline walk, bike) on non-lifting days.
- Don't let cardio eat into lifting recovery — lifting keeps the muscle.

---

## Nutrition (drives 80% of results, especially with appetite suppressed on Reta)
- **Protein: ~150–165 g/day** (≈1 g per lb of goal weight). Protein FIRST at every meal,
  front-loaded earlier in the day when appetite's best. This is what keeps her toned vs. skinny-fat.
- **Eat on a schedule, not on hunger** (Reta kills hunger). 4–5 protein "hits" of ~30 g.
- **Water ~90–100 oz/day + electrolytes** (sodium/potassium/magnesium) — prevents the GLP-1
  fatigue/dizziness/constipation.
- **Fiber** (veg, fruit, oats) ramped up slowly with water — helps constipation.
- **Creatine 5 g/day** — safe, proven for women, helps strength/muscle.
- Keep **some dietary fat** in (avocado, eggs, olive oil) — hormones + gallbladder health.
- **Modest deficit only.** ~1–1.5 lb/week. Crashing it loses muscle and stalls progress.

## How she tracks progress
- **Waist + hip measurements** and **monthly photos** — better than the scale (muscle masks fat loss).
- **Lift log going up** = muscle being kept.
- Scale trending down ~1–1.5 lb/week; a flat week with a shrinking waist is still winning.
