# Training + Nutrition Plan — JR (pairs with peptide phased plan)

**Date:** 2026-05-31
**Summary:** A simple, sustainable resistance-training split and a high-protein meal template
built to preserve muscle through the cut to 185 lb and then drive recomposition (muscle up, fat
down). Designed to pair with the Reta/Tesa/Semax phased dosing plan. Built for someone with a
busy schedule who is not chasing a bodybuilder lifestyle — just real results.

> The peptides set the table; **this is what actually builds the body.** Training tells your
> body to *keep and build* muscle while the deficit/maintenance handles the fat. Protein is the
> raw material. Sleep is when it happens. Miss these and the peptides just make you smaller, not better.

---

## PART 1 — TRAINING

### The plan: 4-day Upper/Lower (each muscle 2×/week — the recomp sweet spot)
If 4 days is too much some weeks, drop to the **3-day full-body** fallback below — never skip
lifting entirely, that's where the muscle protection lives.

**Weekly layout:** Mon Upper · Tue Lower · Thu Upper · Fri Lower (move days as needed, just keep
~48 h between sessions that hit the same muscles).

#### UPPER (A & B alternate, or just repeat — both fine)
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Chest press (DB or machine or bench) | 3 × 6–10 | Main push |
| Row (DB, cable, or machine) | 3 × 8–12 | Main pull |
| Overhead press (DB or machine) | 3 × 8–12 | Shoulders |
| Lat pulldown or pull-up | 3 × 8–12 | Back width |
| Biceps curl | 2 × 10–15 | |
| Triceps pushdown / dip | 2 × 10–15 | |

#### LOWER
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Squat (goblet, machine, or barbell) | 3 × 6–10 | Main legs |
| Romanian deadlift or hip hinge | 3 × 8–12 | Hamstrings/glutes |
| Leg press or split squat | 3 × 10–15 | Quads |
| Leg curl | 2 × 10–15 | Hamstrings |
| Calf raise | 3 × 12–20 | |
| Plank / ab work | 2–3 sets | Core |

#### 3-DAY FULL-BODY FALLBACK (Mon/Wed/Fri)
Each day: 1 squat/leg move · 1 hinge · 1 push · 1 pull · 1 arms/accessory. 3 sets each, 6–12 reps.

### The only progression rule you need
**Progressive overload.** Each session, try to beat last time — one more rep, or a little more
weight. When you hit the *top* of the rep range on all sets, **add weight** next time and drop
back to the bottom of the range. Log it (notes app is fine). This is what forces growth.

- Leave **1–2 reps in the tank** (don't grind to total failure except occasionally on the last set).
- Rest **~1.5–3 min** between hard sets — full recovery beats rushing.
- **Warm up:** 5 min easy cardio + 1–2 light ramp-up sets on your first big lift.

### Cardio / steps
- **8–10k steps/day** for fat loss + heart health (HR matters — you're on Reta).
- Optional 1–2× short zone-2 cardio (20–30 min). **Don't let cardio eat into lifting recovery** —
  lifting is the priority for keeping muscle.

## PART 2 — NUTRITION

### The numbers
- **Protein: 185–200 g/day** (1 g per lb of goal weight) — *non-negotiable, the #1 lever.*
- **Phase 1 (cut to 185):** modest deficit, ~**1–2 lb/week** loss. Don't crash it — slow protects muscle.
- **Phase 2 (recomp at 185):** eat at **maintenance** (scale roughly flat). You build muscle on
  the calories; the training + slight nutrient partitioning lean you out over time.
- **Fat:** keep some in (~0.3–0.4 g/lb) — hormones + gallbladder health (rapid loss = gallstone risk).
- **Carbs:** fill the rest, **most around your workout** for training energy + recovery.

### How to actually hit protein when Reta kills your appetite
1. **Protein FIRST, every meal** — eat it before anything else fills your limited stomach.
2. **Front-load the morning** — your appetite is best early; get 50–70 g before noon.
3. **Eat on the clock, not on hunger** — set 4–5 protein "hits," ~30–45 g each.
4. **Liquids when solids won't go down** — a whey shake is 25–50 g you can sip.
5. **Density over volume** — lean meat, Greek yogurt, eggs, cottage cheese, whey, fish.

### Sample day (~190 g protein) — adjust portions to your calorie phase
| Time | Meal | Protein |
|---|---|---|
| **Wake** | Semax → 3 whole eggs + 3 egg whites + handful spinach | ~30 g |
| **Mid-AM** | Greek yogurt (1 cup) + berries, or a whey shake | ~30 g |
| **Lunch** | 6 oz chicken/turkey/lean beef + rice/potato + veg | ~45 g |
| **Pre/Post lift** | Whey shake (1–2 scoops) | ~30–50 g |
| **Dinner (earlier)** | 6 oz fish/chicken/lean steak + veg + small carb | ~45 g |
| **Total** | | **~180–200 g** |

*(Dinner earlier + lighter on carbs so you finish ≥2 h before your bedtime Tesamorelin — food/insulin blunts the GH pulse.)*

### Daily non-negotiables
- **Water:** ~½ your bodyweight (lb) in oz (~100 oz). Sip, don't chug when queasy.
- **Electrolytes:** sodium/potassium/magnesium daily — kills the "GLP-1 fatigue," dizziness, constipation.
- **Fiber:** ramp **gradually** with water (veg, berries, oats, chia) — too much too fast = bloating.
- **Creatine 5 g/day** — cheapest, most-proven muscle/strength supplement; safe alongside all of this.
- **Sleep 7–9 h** — recovery + the Tesamorelin GH pulse both live here. This is a training variable.

## PART 3 — HOW TO KNOW IT'S WORKING

Track these every 1–2 weeks (not daily-scale obsession):
- **Waist measurement** (navel level) — should trend **down**.
- **Lift logbook** — weights/reps should trend **up** (this = muscle being kept/built).
- **Scale** — Phase 1: slow down. **Phase 2 recomp: flat is success** (fat off, muscle on cancel out).
- **Progress photos** monthly — recomp shows in the mirror before the scale.
- **Body-comp scan (DEXA/InBody) every 8–12 weeks** — confirms fat down, lean mass held/up.

> If the scale stalls at 185 but waist is shrinking and lifts are climbing — **that is the goal,
> not a plateau.** Don't "fix" it with more Reta. Keep eating, keep lifting.
