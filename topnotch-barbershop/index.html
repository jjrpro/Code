Who We Are

Real Craft.
Real People.

Top Notch isn't a chain. It's not a franchise. It's a shop built on the corner of skill and respect — the kind of place where your barber knows your name, your lineup, and when something's off.

We're rooted in Staten Island and we take that seriously. Every cut matters. Every client matters. Whether you're sitting down for a quick edge-up or a full service, you're getting the same attention to detail every single time.

No gimmicks. No corporate nonsense. Just sharp cuts and good people.
