# Shopify Store Audit — JJR Self-Diagnostic

**Store**: jaurxflips.myshopify.com (currently branded "TheOneStopShop", socials @jaurxshops)
**Time to complete**: 30 min
**Use**: open your store in mobile Safari + Shopify Admin in another tab. Score each item Good/Bad. Anything marked Bad → I'll write the fix.

---

## A. BRAND IDENTITY (highest leverage — fix first)

### 1. Single, consistent name across all surfaces
- **Check**: Storefront title, Shopify Admin → Settings → Store details, all social bios, packaging
- **Good**: One name everywhere (recommend: `JaurxShops`)
- **Bad**: Currently THREE — `jaurxflips` (URL), `TheOneStopShop` (brand), `@jaurxshops` (socials)
- **Impact**: 🔴 HIGH — fractured trust kills conversion

### 2. Custom domain (not .myshopify.com)
- **Check**: What URL shows in browser bar on the storefront
- **Good**: `jaurxshops.com` or similar
- **Bad**: `jaurxflips.myshopify.com` (current — looks unprofessional, ads disallow it on some platforms)
- **Impact**: 🔴 HIGH — $10/yr domain is cheapest credibility you'll ever buy

### 3. Logo and favicon present
- **Check**: Top-left of storefront on mobile + tab icon on desktop browser
- **Good**: Crisp logo (PNG or SVG), favicon shows in tab
- **Bad**: Text-only header, default Shopify shopping-bag favicon
- **Impact**: 🟡 MEDIUM

---

## B. HOMEPAGE / ABOVE-THE-FOLD

### 4. Hero clarifies what you sell in 2 seconds
- **Check**: First screen of mobile homepage, before any scroll
- **Good**: "[Product] for [audience]" headline + hero image of the product in use + visible CTA button
- **Bad**: Generic carousel, "Welcome to our store", no clear product shown
- **Impact**: 🔴 HIGH — 70%+ of mobile traffic bounces from a confused hero

### 5. Hero has ONE clear call-to-action
- **Check**: Is there a single button that takes you to a product?
- **Good**: "Shop Magnetic Wallets" or "Get Yours" → goes directly to product or collection
- **Bad**: 3+ buttons, or "Learn More", or no button
- **Impact**: 🟡 MEDIUM

### 6. Featured product or collection above the fold
- **Check**: Without scrolling, can you click straight to a product?
- **Good**: Yes
- **Bad**: Have to scroll past hero + "about us" before seeing anything to buy
- **Impact**: 🟡 MEDIUM

---

## C. PRODUCT PAGES

### 7. At least 4 product photos, lifestyle + flat lay
- **Check**: Tap one product, count images
- **Good**: 4+ photos, mix of product-on-phone shots, hand-held demos, flat lay, packaging
- **Bad**: 1-2 stock AliExpress photos
- **Impact**: 🔴 HIGH — magnetic wallets need to be SEEN in use

### 8. Bullet-point benefits, not just specs
- **Check**: Read the first 3 lines of product description
- **Good**: "Holds 3 cards + cash", "Snaps to any MagSafe case", "Lifetime grip guarantee"
- **Bad**: "100% PU leather. 6.5cm x 9cm. Magnetic." (specs-only is a death sentence)
- **Impact**: 🔴 HIGH

### 9. Price comparison / anchor
- **Check**: Is there a strikethrough "compare at" price?
- **Good**: `$19.99` with `~~$34.99~~` crossed out → shows savings
- **Bad**: Just `$19.99` with no anchor
- **Impact**: 🟡 MEDIUM (works on impulse buys)

### 10. Real reviews visible on product page
- **Check**: Scroll to the bottom of a product page
- **Good**: 10+ reviews with photos (use Loox or Judge.me free tier; can import from AliExpress)
- **Bad**: No reviews, or "Be the first to review"
- **Impact**: 🔴 HIGH — first thing buyers look for

### 11. Trust badges near Add to Cart button
- **Check**: What's right above or below the Buy button?
- **Good**: "Free shipping over $35", "30-day returns", "10,000+ happy customers", payment icons
- **Bad**: Nothing — just the bare button
- **Impact**: 🟡 MEDIUM

---

## D. CART & CHECKOUT

### 12. Cart shows free-shipping threshold progress
- **Check**: Add 1 item, open cart drawer
- **Good**: "Add $15.01 for FREE shipping" progress bar visible
- **Bad**: No mention of free shipping → leaves AOV on the table
- **Impact**: 🟡 MEDIUM (free Shopify apps do this)

### 13. Cart upsell / cross-sell
- **Check**: With 1 item in cart, see if other products are suggested
- **Good**: "Customers also bought" or "Complete the look" with 1-3 items
- **Bad**: No upsell — every upsell shown = +5-15% AOV
- **Impact**: 🟡 MEDIUM

### 14. Express checkout buttons (Shop Pay, Apple Pay)
- **Check**: Cart page on mobile
- **Good**: Big Shop Pay + Apple Pay buttons above the "checkout" button
- **Bad**: Only generic "Checkout" → losing the impulse buyers
- **Impact**: 🟡 MEDIUM

### 15. Post-purchase upsell
- **Check**: Place a fake test order (you can refund yourself), watch what happens AFTER you pay
- **Good**: One-click upsell offer appears ("Add a 2nd wallet, 20% off — one tap")
- **Bad**: Goes straight to thank-you page
- **Impact**: 🔴 HIGH — easiest AOV win, ~10-30% take rate

---

## E. RETENTION / EMAIL

### 16. Email capture popup or footer signup
- **Check**: Wait 10 sec on homepage, check for popup. Scroll to footer.
- **Good**: Popup with 10-15% discount in exchange for email, OR clear footer signup
- **Bad**: Nothing — every visitor who doesn't buy is a lost lead forever
- **Impact**: 🔴 HIGH — Klaviyo free tier handles 250 contacts

### 17. Abandoned checkout email enabled
- **Check**: Shopify Admin → Settings → Notifications → "Abandoned checkout"
- **Good**: Enabled, sends at 1 hour and 10 hours
- **Bad**: Disabled (default on newer stores)
- **Impact**: 🔴 HIGH — 5-15% of abandoned carts recover

---

## F. TECHNICAL / SPEED

### 18. Mobile page speed
- **Check**: Visit https://pagespeed.web.dev/, paste your product page URL
- **Good**: Mobile score 50+, LCP under 3.5s
- **Bad**: Score under 40, LCP over 4s
- **Impact**: 🟡 MEDIUM — speed kills mobile conversion. If bad, check theme bloat + image sizes.
