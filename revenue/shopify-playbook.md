# Shopify Playbook — Magnetic Phone Wallet Dropship Store

**Store**: jaurxflips.myshopify.com (rebrand: JaurxShops recommended)
**Niche**: 7 SKU magnetic MagSafe wallets, $16.99-$24.99
**Stage**: ~$22 ad spend lifetime, social presence on @jaurxshops (TT+IG)
**Goal**: Get the store to a state where every ad dollar can confidently scale

Execute top to bottom. ~3-4 hours of work if you batch it.

---

## PHASE 1 — Brand & Domain (30 min, do TODAY)

### Action 1.1 — Pick one name, kill the others
Three names is killing trust before customers even see a product. Decision:

**Recommended: `JaurxShops`** (matches your TikTok + IG, biggest existing brand equity).

Steps:
1. Shopify Admin → **Settings** → **Store details** → Store name = `JaurxShops`
2. Settings → **Brand** → Logo, slogan ("Magnetic phone wallets that don't fall off."), colors
3. Update all social bios to use `JaurxShops` and point to the new domain

### Action 1.2 — Buy and set primary domain
1. Settings → **Domains** → **Buy new domain**
2. Try `jaurxshops.com` ($14/yr via Shopify), fallback `jaurx.com` if taken
3. Once purchased, **Set as primary**
4. Redirect `jaurxflips.myshopify.com` → new domain (automatic)

**Why this is #1**: Every other improvement multiplies off a coherent brand. Skip this and the rest of this playbook delivers half the impact.

## PHASE 2 — Theme & Homepage (45 min)

### Action 2.1 — Use Dawn (free Shopify theme) if you're on a paid theme
Paid themes are usually slower and harder to customize. Dawn is fast, Shopify-supported, free, and converts as well as anything for stores at your stage.

Online Store → Themes → Free themes → Add Dawn → Customize.

### Action 2.2 — Homepage section order (top to bottom)
1. **Hero** — full-width image of phone with wallet attached, headline + CTA
2. **Featured product** — your top seller, "Add to cart" right there
3. **Press / social proof bar** — "As seen on TikTok @jaurxshops · 50K+ views" (use real numbers)
4. **Featured collection** — all 7 wallets, grid layout
5. **Image-text block** — "Why JaurxShops" (3 benefit columns: Strong magnets, MagSafe compatible, Free US shipping)
6. **Reviews section** (powered by Loox/Judge.me — install in Phase 4)
7. **FAQ collapsible** — 5 common questions (compatibility, shipping time, returns, magnets vs cases, card capacity)
8. **Email signup** — "10% off first order"
9. **Footer** — policies, contact, social

### Action 2.3 — Hero copy template
```
HEADLINE:    Magnetic Wallets That Actually Stick.
SUBHEAD:     Snap-on MagSafe wallets for iPhone 12 and up.
             Holds 3 cards + cash. Won't fall off.
CTA BUTTON:  Shop Wallets — From $16.99
```

Mobile-first: keep the headline ≤ 6 words, subhead ≤ 12 words. Long headers tank conversion on phones.

### Action 2.4 — Hero photo
Don't use stock AliExpress photos for the hero. Either:
- Shoot 1 photo yourself: white wall, hand holding iPhone, wallet snapped on back, good lighting
- Or use DSers' "Lifestyle" image filter to find real lifestyle shots, NOT product-on-white-background

Hero image is 60% of first impression. Spend 10 min on this.

## PHASE 3 — Product Pages (60 min — biggest revenue lever)

### Action 3.1 — Pricing decision

Look at your 7 products honestly. Are they:

**(A) Functionally identical, just different colors/patterns?**
→ Price them ALL at $19.99. Tiered pricing only works when there's clear product hierarchy.

**(B) Different card capacities, materials, or features?**
→ Keep tiered, but explain it on the page: "3-card slim" $16.99, "5-card with cash pocket" $19.99, "Leather + RFID-blocking" $24.99

If you're unsure, default to (A). Simpler beats clever every time at $20 price points.

### Action 3.2 — Product page section order
1. **5-6 product photos** (see Action 3.3)
2. **Product title** (rewrite — see Action 3.4)
3. **Price** with compare-at strikethrough for perceived discount
4. **Star rating + review count** (from Loox)
5. **Color/style variant picker** (if applicable)
6. **Add to cart button** — full width, bold color, "ADD TO CART · $19.99"
7. **Trust strip** below button: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 1,200+ Happy Customers · Free US Shipping Over $35 · 30-Day Returns
8. **Benefit bullets** (5-7 lines, see Action 3.5)
9. **How it works** — 3-step graphic: Peel · Stick · Snap
10. **FAQ accordion** specific to wallets
11. **Reviews section** — full Loox widget with photos
12. **Related products** — other 6 wallets

### Action 3.3 — Photo requirements per product
You need 5-6 photos. Order matters:
1. **Hero**: clean product shot on solid background (DSers stock OK)
2. **Lifestyle**: wallet on phone in hand or pocket
3. **Detail**: cards being inserted, showing capacity
4. **Magnet demo**: phone hanging upside down or wallet sticking to fridge (proves the grip)
5. **Sizing**: wallet next to ruler or comparison object
6. **Packaging** (optional): shows it's not just a baggie of AliExpress junk

Pull 3-4 from DSers, shoot 1-2 yourself with your phone.

### Action 3.4 — Title rewrite formula
**Bad** (default AliExpress import): `Magnetic Card Holder PU Leather Wallet for iPhone MagSafe Slim Mini Pocket`
**Good**: `JaurxShops MagSafe Wallet — Slim Card Holder for iPhone 12-15 Pro Max`

Formula: `[Brand] [Product Type] — [Key Benefit] [Compatibility]`

### Action 3.5 — Benefit bullets (NOT features)
```
✓ Snaps on instantly — works with any MagSafe case or naked iPhone
✓ Holds up to 3 cards + folded cash, stays slim in your pocket
✓ Strong N52 magnets — won't slide, won't fall off (tested at 30 MPH)
✓ Premium PU leather — feels like the real thing for half the price
✓ Works with iPhone 12, 13, 14, 15 and the Pro / Pro Max lineup
✓ Ships from US warehouse — most orders arrive in 5-7 days
```

Each bullet: benefit first, spec second. "Strong magnets so it won't fall off" not "N52 neodymium magnets, 1.2T magnetic flux."

### Action 3.6 — Description body
Below the bullets, add 3 short paragraphs:

```
**The wallet your phone has been waiting for.**
Tired of carrying both a wallet and a phone? Tired of cases that
make your phone too thick? Our magnetic MagSafe wallet snaps
on in seconds and holds the essentials — your ID, debit card,
and a couple of bills — without adding bulk.

**Built to actually stay attached.**
We tested cheap magnetic wallets so you don't have to. Most of them
slide off when you grab your phone from your pocket. Ours uses
N52-grade magnets — the same strength Apple uses in MagSafe
chargers — so once it's on, it stays on.

**Stop juggling a wallet, a phone, and a card case.**
Slip your ID and primary card into the wallet. Snap it to the back
of your phone. Out the door. You'll wonder how you carried
everything separately before.
```

Hits all 3 buyer psychology levers: problem (juggling), competitor takedown (others fall off), end state (frictionless life).

## PHASE 4 — Apps Stack (45 min total install + setup)

Install in this order. Total monthly cost: $0-30 depending on tier.

### 4.1 — Reviews: **Loox** ($9.99/mo trial, then free if under 100 orders)
- Apps → search "Loox" → install
- Import reviews: Loox has a built-in AliExpress importer. Pull 10-15 reviews per product, MIX OF 4 AND 5 STAR (all-5 looks fake).
- Enable "review request emails" (sends 14 days post-purchase)
- Place review widget on product pages + a homepage widget showing 6 latest reviews

### 4.2 — Email: **Klaviyo** (free up to 250 contacts, 500 emails/mo)
- Apps → Klaviyo → install → connect Shopify
- Create these 3 flows (Klaviyo has templates):
  - **Welcome flow** (3 emails): triggered on email signup → 10% off, brand story, best-sellers
  - **Abandoned checkout** (2 emails): 1 hour after abandon → reminder + free shipping nudge. 24 hours → 10% off code.
  - **Post-purchase thank you** (2 emails): immediate confirmation, day 14 review request
- Add a signup popup with delay 8 sec, exit-intent enabled

### 4.3 — Upsell: **ReConvert** (free up to 50 orders/mo)
- Apps → ReConvert → install
- Create 1 post-purchase upsell: "Add a 2nd JaurxShops wallet — 25% off, one-tap add"
- Place on thank-you page

### 4.4 — Cart drawer / free shipping bar: **Beeketing Free Shipping Bar** (free)
OR just enable Shopify's native cart drawer in theme settings.
- Set threshold: **$35** (forces ATV up since lowest product is $16.99 — they add a 2nd one)
- Bar text: "🚚 You're $X away from FREE shipping!"

### 4.5 — Trust badges: built into Dawn
- Customize theme → Product → Add "Trust badges" block under Add to Cart
- Use: Free Shipping · 30-Day Returns · Secure Checkout · Money-Back Guarantee

## PHASE 5 — Settings & Policies (30 min)

### 5.1 — Shipping
- Settings → Shipping → Add zone "United States"
- Add rate: **Free shipping for orders $35+**
- Add rate: **$4.99 flat shipping under $35**
- DSers will fulfill via ePacket or DHL — set processing time to **"2 business days"** to set realistic expectations

### 5.2 — Policies (Shopify auto-generates templates — USE THEM)
- Settings → Policies → tap each one → "Insert default template"
- Edit only the company name field. Don't write your own legalese.
- Required: Refund, Shipping, Privacy, Terms of Service

### 5.3 — Notifications
- Settings → Notifications → **Abandoned checkout** → ON
- Customize the email subject: "Forgot something? Here's 10% off."
- Use a discount code (CART10) that you create in Discounts

### 5.4 — Checkout
- Settings → Checkout → Customer accounts → **Optional** (forcing signup tanks conversion)
- Shop Pay → **Enabled**
- Apple Pay / Google Pay → **Enabled**

## PHASE 6 — Tracking Verification (15 min — CRITICAL before scaling ads)

### 6.1 — TikTok Pixel
- Apps → TikTok → install/open → Conversion API → connect
- Use Chrome ext **TikTok Pixel Helper** → visit your site → verify pixel fires `ViewContent`, `AddToCart`, `InitiateCheckout`, `Purchase`

### 6.2 — Meta Pixel + CAPI
- Apps → Facebook & Instagram → install → Conversion API → set up
- Use **Meta Pixel Helper** Chrome ext → repeat above 4 events

### 6.3 — Google Analytics 4
- Settings → Customer events → Google Analytics 4 → connect
- Worth having even if you're not running Google Ads — gives you real session/conversion data

## PHASE 7 — Pre-Scale Final Check (10 min)

Before pushing ad budget past $50/day, verify:

- [ ] Custom domain live, all redirects working
- [ ] Mobile homepage loads in under 3.5 sec (test at pagespeed.web.dev)
- [ ] You can complete a full checkout on mobile in under 90 sec
- [ ] Pixel/CAPI fires Purchase event on test order
- [ ] Reviews show on at least 5 of 7 product pages (10+ reviews each)
- [ ] Email signup popup appears within 10 sec on first visit
- [ ] Abandoned checkout email arrives if you abandon a test cart
- [ ] Post-purchase upsell appears after test checkout
- [ ] Free shipping bar shows in cart drawer

When all 9 are ✅, the store is "ad-ready." Then crank to the budget ladder in `SHOPIFY-ADS.md`.
