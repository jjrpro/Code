# Faceless AI Video Channel — Operating Blueprint

Prepared for JR · May 31, 2026

This is the full plan for a hands-off, AI-produced faceless video channel built to actually clear monetization in 2026 — not get flagged as low-effort AI content. It splits cleanly into **what Claude runs** and **what only you can do** (accounts + money).

---

## 1. The honest model

There is no version of this where "Claude pays you weekly." I can't hold money or own accounts. What we're building instead is a machine **you own**, where:

- **Claude** generates the videos, scripts, titles, thumbnails, and schedules — the labor.
- **The platforms** (YouTube/TikTok) pay **you** directly, into **your** AdSense/creator account linked to **your** bank.

Payouts are **monthly**, not weekly, and only after a ramp. AdSense pays around the 21st of each month once you've cleared a $100 balance. So the realistic target is a first deposit a few months out, then a growing monthly amount — not week-one cash.

---

## 2. Why most AI channels die in 2026 (and how we avoid it)

YouTube renamed its "repetitious content" rule to **"inauthentic content"** and now actively rejects: AI voiceovers slapped over stock footage, recycled clips, and low-effort AI compilations. Meeting the subscriber numbers is not enough — the content has to add genuine value through original writing, transformation, or editing.

**Our defense:** every video is built on an **original written script** (real research, real angle) and **original AI-generated cinematic visuals** — not scraped stock. That combination is exactly the "transformation + value" YouTube wants to see. This is the single most important design choice in the whole plan.

---

## 3. Recommended niche: "Cinematic Curiosities"

**Format:** 60–90 second mini-documentaries, each answering one genuinely fascinating question — space, deep ocean, history's mysteries, the human body, future tech.

**Why this niche:**

- Plays to AI video's biggest strength: gorgeous cinematic visuals you can't easily film.
- Evergreen and broad — never runs out of topics, and a hit can resurface for years.
- Works as both YouTube Shorts (9:16) **and** longer YouTube uploads (16:9), so we can chase either monetization path.
- Original script + original visuals = passes the 2026 authenticity bar.
- High "rewatch + share" rate, which the Shorts algorithm rewards.

**Channel identity (draft):**

- Name ideas: *Curious Frame*, *The Unseen Reel*, *Wonder Index*, *90 Second Universe*.
- Voice: calm, confident, slightly cinematic narrator.
- Visual signature: consistent color grade, same intro stinger, same caption font.

*(Swap the niche if you prefer — luxury/architecture, calm nature, or "how things work" all work with the same pipeline.)*

## 4. Setup checklist — YOUR one-time tasks

These require your identity and bank, so they're yours. Each is quick; I'll walk you through any of them.

1. Create a dedicated **Google account** for the channel (keep it separate from personal).
2. Create the **YouTube channel**; add banner, profile, and a clear "About" description.
3. Turn on **2-Step Verification** (required for monetization).
4. Create a **TikTok account** with the same name/branding.
5. When eligible, link an **AdSense account** with your real legal name + bank for payouts.
6. (Optional, recommended) Authorize a **social scheduler** (e.g., a tool that posts on a timer) so even publishing becomes hands-off and rule-compliant.

The channel must be **at least 6 months old** before full ad monetization is approved — so the earlier you create it, the better. Do this part this week even before we have a single video.

## 5. The production pipeline — what Claude runs

For each video, automatically:

1. **Pick the topic** from the content calendar (or generate a fresh one).
2. **Write the script** — a tight, researched 120–180 word narration with a 2-second hook.
3. **Generate the visuals** — cinematic AI clips matched to each script beat (9:16 for Shorts).
4. **Write the package** — title, description, 15–20 hashtags, and a thumbnail concept.
5. **Score it** — run virality prediction and keep only the strongest before it's queued.
6. **Drop it in your folder** — finished video + a ready-to-paste publish sheet.

You (or your scheduler) hit publish. That's the only manual touch.

## 7. Monetization milestones & realistic math

**The thresholds (verified, 2026):**

- **Fan-funding tier:** 500 subscribers + 3,000 watch hours (12 mo) *or* 3M Shorts views (90 days) + 3 public uploads in 90 days. Unlocks Super Thanks/memberships — **no ad revenue**.
- **Full ad revenue:** 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours (12 mo) *or* 10M Shorts views (90 days). Channel must be 6+ months old. Approval takes 1–4 weeks after you apply.

**Rough Shorts economics:** Shorts pay roughly $0.03–$0.10 per 1,000 views (RPM), so monetization is a **volume game**. Long-form mini-docs pay far higher RPM but need watch hours. Smart move: post Shorts daily for reach, and compile/extend the best into longer uploads to bank watch hours toward the higher-paying path.

**Realistic timeline (1–2 quality videos/day, no guarantees):**

- **Month 1–2:** Build catalog, find what hooks. Expect slow numbers. $0.
- **Month 3–4:** One or two videos catch the algorithm; subs climb. Possibly cross the fan-funding tier.
- **Month 5–7:** Cross 1,000 subs + view threshold; apply for full YPP. First AdSense deposit lands the month after you clear $100.
- **Beyond:** Income scales with catalog size and hit rate. A channel posting consistently for a year with a few breakout videos is the goal — not overnight.

Anyone promising faster is selling something.
