# Day 8 — The sound that no one could explain (The Bloop)

**Angle:** Audio mystery · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
In 1997, the ocean made a sound louder than a blue whale — and nothing could explain it.

**BODY:**
It was picked up by sensors thousands of miles apart: an ultra-low-frequency
rumble nicknamed "the Bloop." It was so powerful that for years no known animal
could account for it. People imagined a creature larger than anything we'd ever
seen, hiding in the deep. The likely answer turned out to be just as dramatic —
an icequake: a massive Antarctic glacier cracking and breaking apart. But for a
while, the ocean had everyone guessing.
