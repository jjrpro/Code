# Day 27 — What's the brightest thing in the universe?

**Angle:** Quasar reveal · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
The brightest objects in the universe are powered by black holes eating.

**BODY:**
They're called quasars. At the center of some galaxies, a giant black hole pulls
in matter so fast that it heats up and blazes — outshining its entire galaxy of
hundreds of billions of stars. The brightest quasars are trillions of times more
luminous than our sun. They're so far away that their light has traveled for
billions of years to reach us — so we see them as they were when the universe was
young.

**PAYOFF:**
The brightest lights in existence come from the darkest objects we know.
