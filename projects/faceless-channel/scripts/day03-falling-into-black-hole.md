# Day 3 — What would happen if you fell into a black hole?

**Angle:** Spaghettification, visualized · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
You wouldn't be crushed. You'd be stretched into a strand of atoms.

**BODY:**
As you fall toward a black hole, gravity pulls your feet far harder than your
head — stretching you thinner and thinner. Scientists call it spaghettification.
But here's the strange part: to someone watching from far away, you'd never
actually fall in. You'd appear to freeze at the edge, glowing red, slowing
forever — because near a black hole, time itself bends. Two completely different
endings, for the same fall.

**PAYOFF:**
Both are real.
