# Day 18 — What would the last day on Earth look like?

**Angle:** Sun's red-giant phase · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
In billions of years, the sun will swallow the Earth — and we know roughly how.

**BODY:**
As the sun ages, it will run out of hydrogen and swell into a red giant. It will
grow hundreds of times larger, swallowing Mercury, then Venus. Earth's oceans
will boil away, the sky will glow red, and the surface will melt. Our planet may
be dragged in entirely. It's not a guess — it's the well-understood life cycle of
a star like ours. We just have about five billion years before the show.

**PAYOFF:**
Everything here has an expiration date — written into the sun itself.
