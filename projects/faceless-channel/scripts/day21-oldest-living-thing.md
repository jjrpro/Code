# Day 21 — What's the oldest living thing on Earth?

**Angle:** 5,000-year-old organism · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
There's a tree alive today that was already ancient when the pyramids were built.

**BODY:**
High in California's White Mountains grows a bristlecone pine nicknamed
Methuselah — nearly 5,000 years old. It was a seedling before writing spread,
before Stonehenge was finished. It survives by growing painfully slowly in
brutal, dry, freezing conditions that kill almost everything else. Its exact
location is kept secret to protect it from the world.

**PAYOFF:**
A single living thing has quietly watched all of recorded history go by.
