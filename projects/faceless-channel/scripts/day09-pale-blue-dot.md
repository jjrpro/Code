# Day 9 — What does Earth look like from the edge of the solar system?

**Angle:** Pale blue dot zoom-out · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~75s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
In 1990, a spacecraft turned around and took our portrait.

**BODY:**
Voyager 1 was nearly four billion miles away when it pointed its camera back
home. In the photo, Earth isn't a globe. It's a single pale blue dot — less than
a pixel, suspended in a beam of scattered sunlight. Everyone who ever lived,
every war, every love story, every empire — all of it, on that one speck. From
out there, the thing that feels so enormous to us is almost nothing at all.

**PAYOFF:**
That photo changed how a generation saw itself.
