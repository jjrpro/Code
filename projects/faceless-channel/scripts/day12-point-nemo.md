# Day 12 — What's the loneliest place on Earth?

**Angle:** Point Nemo · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
There's a spot in the ocean where the nearest humans are in space.

**BODY:**
It's called Point Nemo — the point in the Pacific farthest from any land. It's so
remote that the closest people are often the astronauts on the Space Station
passing overhead, just 250 miles up. The nearest land is more than 1,600 miles
away in every direction. It's where space agencies deliberately crash old
satellites, because there's no one around for thousands of miles — a spacecraft
graveyard in the emptiest water on Earth.

**PAYOFF:**
The loneliest place on the planet is easier to reach from orbit.
