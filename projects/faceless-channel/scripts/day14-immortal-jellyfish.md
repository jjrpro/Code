# Day 14 — The animal that doesn't age

**Angle:** Biology hook · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
There's a jellyfish that can hit reverse on death.

**BODY:**
It's called Turritopsis dohrnii — the "immortal jellyfish." When it's injured,
starving, or old, it doesn't die. It transforms its cells back into an earlier
stage of life — like a butterfly turning back into a caterpillar — and starts
over. In theory it can do this again and again, escaping old age entirely. In the
wild, predators still get most of them. But biologically, this tiny creature has
cracked something we can't.

**PAYOFF:**
Somewhere in the ocean is an animal that simply refuses to grow old.
