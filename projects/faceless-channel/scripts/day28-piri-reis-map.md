# Day 28 — The map that knew too much

**Angle:** History · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
A map from 1513 shows coastlines that hadn't officially been discovered yet.

**BODY:**
Old maps are full of mysteries — coastlines drawn with strange accuracy, lands
sketched before explorers reached them. The famous Piri Reis map of 1513 shows
the coast of South America in surprising detail, and some argue it even hints at
the shape of Antarctica beneath its ice — though scholars debate how much is real
accuracy versus lucky guesswork. What's clear is that knowledge traveled by trade
and rumor far more than we tend to assume.

**PAYOFF:**
Sometimes the world was mapped by people history forgot to credit.
