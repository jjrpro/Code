# Day 20 — The lost color humans can't normally see

**Angle:** Perception · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
There may be colors your eyes can see — that your brain refuses to show you.

**BODY:**
Normally, red and green signals in your eyes cancel each other out — that's why
you never see "reddish green." But in rare experiments, when scientists carefully
held an image perfectly still on the retina, some people reported impossible
colors they couldn't describe — hues outside the normal rainbow. And other
animals leave us in the dust: the mantis shrimp has up to sixteen color
receptors, to our three.

**PAYOFF:**
The rainbow you see may be only a sliver of what's really there.
