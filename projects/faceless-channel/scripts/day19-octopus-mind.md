# Day 19 — How do octopuses think?

**Angle:** Distributed intelligence · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
An octopus has nine brains — and most of them are in its arms.

**BODY:**
Two-thirds of an octopus's neurons aren't in its head; they're spread through its
arms. Each arm can taste, touch, and make decisions somewhat on its own. Cut off
from the central brain, an arm can still reach for food. It's a completely
different design from ours — intelligence distributed across the body instead of
locked in one place. Octopuses solve puzzles, open jars, and seem to recognize
individual people.

**PAYOFF:**
It's the closest thing to an alien mind we've ever met — and it lives in our oceans.
