# Day 15 — What's hiding under Antarctica's ice?

**Angle:** Subglacial lakes · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~75s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
Beneath miles of Antarctic ice, there are hidden lakes that never freeze.

**BODY:**
Under the frozen surface lie hundreds of liquid lakes, kept from freezing by the
planet's own heat and the crushing pressure of the ice above. The largest, Lake
Vostok, has been sealed off from the world for millions of years. When scientists
finally drilled down, they found signs of life — microbes evolving in total
darkness, cut off from everything we know. It may be the closest thing on Earth
to the oceans we suspect lie beneath the ice of distant moons.

**PAYOFF:**
The most isolated ecosystem on Earth has been hiding under our feet.
