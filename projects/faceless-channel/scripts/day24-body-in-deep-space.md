# Day 24 — What happens to your body in deep space?

**Angle:** Survival science · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
Without a suit, space would kill you in under two minutes — but not by exploding.

**BODY:**
Movies get it wrong: you wouldn't pop. Your skin would hold you together. But the
air would rush out of your lungs, and the lack of pressure would make the water
in your body start to turn to vapor. You'd have maybe 15 seconds of useful
consciousness before the lack of oxygen took you. The cold is almost an
afterthought — with no air around you, heat actually leaves the body slowly.

**PAYOFF:**
The thing keeping you alive isn't just warmth or air — it's pressure.
