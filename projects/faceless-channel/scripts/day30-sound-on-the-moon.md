# Day 30 — What's the last sound you'd hear on the Moon?

**Angle:** Silence payoff · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
If you shouted on the Moon, the person right next to you would hear nothing.

**BODY:**
The Moon has almost no atmosphere — no air to carry sound. So out in the open
there's perfect silence; even a rocket landing beside you would be soundless.
That's why the astronauts who walked there talked through radios. But there's a
twist: sound can still travel through solids. Press your helmet against a metal
rail, and the vibrations could reach you.

**PAYOFF:**
On the Moon, silence isn't empty — you just have to feel it instead of hear it.
