# Day 1 — What's at the bottom of the ocean's deepest point?

**Angle:** Descent POV to the Mariana Trench · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~75s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
Seven miles down, the pressure could crush a tank like a soda can.

**BODY:**
This is the Challenger Deep — the lowest point on Earth. It's deeper than
Everest is tall. Sunlight never reaches it. The water is just above freezing,
and the pressure is over a thousand times what you feel right now. And yet —
there's life. Tiny creatures thrive here, feeding on chemicals instead of light.
We've sent more people to the Moon than to the bottom of this trench. Most of it
has never been seen by human eyes.

**PAYOFF:**
The deepest part of our own planet is still, mostly, a mystery.
