# Day 6 — What's inside the Great Pyramid we still can't reach?

**Angle:** Sealed-chamber hook · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~75s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
In 2017, scientists found a hidden room in the Great Pyramid — and still can't get in.

**BODY:**
Using cosmic-ray particles called muons, researchers scanned the Great Pyramid of
Giza and discovered a huge empty space above the Grand Gallery — at least 30
meters long, sealed for 4,500 years. No one knows what it is. A hidden chamber? A
gap left by the builders? A burial space never found? There's no known passage to
it. We've mapped the surface of Mars more thoroughly than the inside of a
structure built before the wheel even reached Egypt.

**PAYOFF:**
One of the oldest buildings on Earth still has rooms we've never entered.
