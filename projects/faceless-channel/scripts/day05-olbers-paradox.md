# Day 5 — Why is the night sky dark? (Olbers' paradox)

**Angle:** Cosmic puzzle · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
If the universe is infinite, the night sky should be blinding.

**BODY:**
Here's the puzzle: if there are infinite stars in every direction, every line of
sight should eventually land on one. The whole sky should glow like the surface
of the sun. So why is it dark? The answer is time. The universe isn't infinitely
old — it's about 13.8 billion years. Light from the most distant stars hasn't
reached us yet. And the universe is expanding, stretching that far-off light
until it fades from view.

**PAYOFF:**
The night sky is dark because the universe had a beginning.
