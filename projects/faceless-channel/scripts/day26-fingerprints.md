# Day 26 — Why do we have fingerprints?

**Angle:** Body science · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
Your fingerprints aren't for grip — and no one fully agrees what they're for.

**BODY:**
For years people assumed fingerprints help us grip things. But tests suggest the
ridges may actually reduce friction on smooth surfaces. The leading newer idea:
those tiny ridges amplify vibrations as you touch things, sharpening your sense of
touch so you can feel fine texture. And every print is unique — even between
identical twins — formed by random pressures while you were still in the womb.

**PAYOFF:**
The pattern on your fingertip is yours alone — and we're still decoding why it's there.
