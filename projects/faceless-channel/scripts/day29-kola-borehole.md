# Day 29 — How deep can we actually dig?

**Angle:** Kola Superdeep Borehole · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
We tried to dig to the center of the Earth — and barely scratched it.

**BODY:**
The deepest hole humans ever drilled is the Kola Superdeep Borehole in Russia —
about 7.6 miles down. It took nearly 20 years. And it's almost nothing: only a
fraction of the way through the crust, not even close to the mantle below. They
finally stopped because the rock got too hot — over 180 degrees Celsius, hotter
than expected, and so warm it behaved more like plastic than solid stone.

**PAYOFF:**
We can fly to other planets — but we've barely dented our own.
