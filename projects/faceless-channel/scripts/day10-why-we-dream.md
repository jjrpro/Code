# Day 10 — Why do we dream?

**Angle:** Brain science · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
You'll spend about six years of your life dreaming — and no one's sure why.

**BODY:**
Every night your brain builds entire worlds while your body lies still. One
theory says dreams help process emotions, filing away the day's experiences.
Another says they're rehearsal — practicing threats and social situations in a
safe simulation. Some scientists think dreaming keeps the brain's visual system
sharp through the long dark hours of sleep. We do know it matters: deprived of
dream sleep, people struggle to think and to feel.

**PAYOFF:**
Every night, your mind runs a story it won't let you remember.

Follow for one new wonder a day.
