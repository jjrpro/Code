# Day 13 — How big is the universe, really?

**Angle:** Scale visualization · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
If the sun were a grain of sand, the galaxy would still be wider than a continent.

**BODY:**
Our brains can't hold cosmic scale, so try this. Shrink the sun to a single grain
of sand. On that scale, the nearest star is still miles away. The Milky Way —
billions of stars — would stretch wider than the United States. And the Milky Way
is just one of an estimated two trillion galaxies. There are more stars in the
universe than grains of sand on every beach on Earth.

**PAYOFF:**
We live on one grain, orbiting one speck, in an ocean we can't measure.
