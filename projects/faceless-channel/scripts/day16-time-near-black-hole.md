# Day 16 — Why does time slow down near a black hole?

**Angle:** Relativity, simply · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
Spend an hour near a black hole, and years could pass back home.

**BODY:**
Gravity doesn't just pull on objects — it pulls on time itself. The stronger the
gravity, the slower time runs. Near a black hole, gravity is so extreme that time
crawls compared to the rest of the universe. To you, your watch ticks normally.
But to someone far away, you'd appear almost frozen. It's not science fiction —
it's measurable. Even on Earth, clocks at sea level run slightly slower than
clocks on mountaintops.

**PAYOFF:**
Time isn't fixed. It bends around everything with mass — including you.
