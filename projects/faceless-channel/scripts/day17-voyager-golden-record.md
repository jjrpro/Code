# Day 17 — The message we sent to aliens in 1977

**Angle:** Voyager Golden Record · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~75s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
There's a golden record flying out of the solar system with directions to Earth.

**BODY:**
In 1977, NASA bolted a gold-plated record onto the Voyager probes. On it:
greetings in 55 languages, the sound of a heartbeat, a baby's cry, music from
Bach to Chuck Berry, and images of life on Earth. There's even a map showing
where we are, using pulsars as landmarks. Voyager is now billions of miles away
in interstellar space, and the record is built to last a billion years.

**PAYOFF:**
Long after we're gone, a message saying "we were here" will still be traveling.
