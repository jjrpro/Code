# Day 11 — The city that vanished overnight

**Angle:** Maya collapse (Copán) · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~75s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
A whole city was abandoned in a single day — and no one knows why.

**BODY:**
Around 1,200 years ago, the Maya city of Copán and others across the region were
thriving — grand pyramids, dense populations, advanced astronomy. Then, within a
few generations, many were simply abandoned. Plazas emptied. The jungle moved in.
For a long time it looked like a city vanishing overnight. The likely culprits:
prolonged drought, overfarming, and political collapse stacking on top of each
other.

**PAYOFF:**
Even the most advanced civilizations can unravel when the environment turns against them.
