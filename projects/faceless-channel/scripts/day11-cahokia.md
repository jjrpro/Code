# Day 11 — The city that vanished overnight

**Angle:** History · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~70s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
A thousand years ago, North America's biggest city emptied — and no one knows why.

**BODY:**
Cahokia, near modern St. Louis, was once bigger than London. Tens of thousands of
people, giant earthen pyramids, a planned grid of plazas. Then, within a couple
of generations, it emptied. Drought, flooding, political collapse, disease —
theories abound, but no single cause is proven. The people dispersed, and the
great city of mounds was left to the grass.

**PAYOFF:**
An entire civilization rose here — and then walked away.
