# Day 23 — The door that's been sealed for 1,000 years

**Angle:** Tutankhamun & the "pharaoh's curse" · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~75s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
Some tombs have warnings carved over the door — and we open them anyway.

**BODY:**
Across the ancient world, builders sealed tombs and chambers meant never to be
reopened — some with curses carved above the entrance to scare off intruders.
When archaeologists opened the tomb of Tutankhamun in 1922, the "curse of the
pharaohs" became legend after several people connected to the dig died. The real
explanation was likely mundane — coincidence, and possibly mold and bacteria
sealed inside for millennia. But the mystery endures.

**PAYOFF:**
Every sealed door from the ancient world is a time capsule, holding air no one has breathed in a thousand years.
