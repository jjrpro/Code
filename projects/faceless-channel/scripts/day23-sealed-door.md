# Day 23 — The tomb we've chosen never to open

**Angle:** History mystery · **Ratio:** 9:16 · **Length:** ~75s

**HOOK (0–2s):**
We know where China's first emperor is buried — and we refuse to open it.

**BODY:**
The tomb of Qin Shi Huang has been sealed for over 2,000 years, guarded by the
famous Terracotta Army. Ancient texts say it holds rivers of mercury flowing
beneath a map of his empire — and soil tests around the mound really do show
mercury levels far above normal. So why not dig in? Because excavation could
destroy whatever's inside, and we don't yet have the technology to protect it. So
we wait.

**PAYOFF:**
Sometimes a mystery is protected not by a lock — but by our own caution.
