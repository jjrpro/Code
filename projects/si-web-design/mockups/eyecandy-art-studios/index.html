The Artist

Gene Wagner

New York born and raised, Gene makes art from the heart — rich, expressive oils layered with 24kt gold leaf and genuine gemstones, alive with color and texture you can feel.

Based in Staten Island, his studio is also his home: a sailboat where he paints alongside his wife and their dog, Mojo. Every canvas is original, one of a kind, made with love.
