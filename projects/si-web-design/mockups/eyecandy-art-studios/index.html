The Artist

Gene Wagner

New York born and raised, Gene Wagner makes art from the heart. Working in rich, expressive oils, he brings lions, angels, and fairies to life with color and texture you can feel.

His studio is also his home — a sailboat in New York, where he paints alongside his wife and their dog, Mojo. Every piece is original, one of a kind, and made with love.
