## Objection cheat-sheet
Full list in `sales/02-outreach-scripts.md`. Top 3:
- *"We're fine with Instagram."* → "This works *with* your IG but puts you on Google when people search '[service] near me' — walk-ins you're missing now."
- *"How much?"* → "$400 on your own web address, or $400 + $40/mo hosted. No contract."
- *"I'll think about it."* → "The mockup's yours — I'll check back Friday. Send your logo and I'll drop it in so you see the real thing."
