# 🎉 New Sale — Client Onboarding Checklist

Paste-ready. The second a client says "yes," send them the **client message**
below, then work the **your steps** list. Full detail in `06-preview-to-live-site.md`.

---

## 📋 Message to send the client (copy/paste, fill the [brackets])

> Awesome — so excited to build this for you! 🙌 To get [Business Name] live this
> week, can you send me a few things whenever you get a sec:
>
> 1. **Your logo** (any image is fine — even a photo of your sign)
> 2. **3–8 photos** — storefront, your work/products, the team, inside
> 3. **Best phone + email** for customers
> 4. **Address & hours** (if customers come to you)
> 5. **Your services / menu** (and prices if you want them shown)
> 6. **A sentence or two about the business** — or I'll write it and you approve
> 7. **Do you already own a web address (domain)?** Yes/No
>
> That's everything I need. Once it's live I'll send you the link to look over
> before anything goes public. Talk soon!

---

## 💰 Money (lock this BEFORE building further)

- Quote: **$400 one-time** (live on their own domain), OR **$400 + $40/mo Care Plan**
  (you host + handle edits). Push the Care Plan — recurring income.
- Take a **deposit (50% or full)** before pointing a domain live.
- Collect via: Stripe Payment Link / Square / Zelle / Venmo.
- Optional closer: "First few Staten Island businesses get the build at $250."

## 🛠️ Your steps once they send their stuff

- [ ] Send me (Claude) everything they gave you → I finalize their mockup with real info + photos
- [ ] Domain: buy at Namecheap/Porkbun (~$12/yr) **in the client's name** (they own it)
- [ ] Deploy final folder to Netlify → connect domain → SSL auto/free
- [ ] Set up their **Google Business Profile** (free, 15 min — big value-add)
- [ ] Test on your phone: call button dials, map right, hours correct, form emails them
- [ ] Send live link for approval → collect balance
- [ ] Hand off + "text me for changes" → **ask for a review of your service**
- [ ] Log as WON in tracker (domain + plan)
