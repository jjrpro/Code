# Outreach Scripts — paste-ready

The winning move: **build a free 1-screen mockup of THEIR site first, then reach
out.** "I already made you something" beats "do you want to buy something" every
time. Host the mockup free on Netlify Drop and send the link.

Keep it personal. Use their real name, mention something specific (their reviews,
a dish, a recent job). Generic = ignored.

---

## A. Cold email / Facebook DM (mockup already built)

> **Subject:** Made you a quick website mockup, [Business Name] 👀
>
> Hi [Owner Name],
>
> I'm [Your Name] — I'm local, [neighborhood] — and I build simple, modern
> websites for Staten Island businesses. I noticed [Business Name] doesn't have
> a website yet (just the Facebook page), so I went ahead and built you a quick
> mockup to show what it could look like:
>
> 👉 [your-netlify-link.netlify.app]
>
> It's mobile-friendly, has your hours, a "call now" button, and a spot for your
> menu/services. No obligation — I just thought you'd want to see it.
>
> If you like it, I can have it live on your own domain this week. Happy to show
> you how it works whenever's good.
>
> — [Your Name], Jaurx Web Design
> [phone] · [email]

---

## B. Walk-in / in-person (the strongest channel on SI)

Print the mockup or pull it up on your phone. Walk in during a slow hour.

> "Hey, is the owner around? … Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name], I'm local. I build
> websites for businesses around here and I actually made a quick mockup for
> [Business Name] — mind if I show you real quick on my phone?"
>
> *(show it — let them react)*
>
> "It's mobile-friendly, customers can tap to call you, see your hours, your
> [menu/services]. I can get this live on your own web address this week. I do
> a one-time build for [$X] and then it's [$Y/mo] if you want me to host it and
> keep it updated — or you can just take it and I'm out of your hair."

Then **stop talking.** Let them respond.

---

## C. Phone call (if no email/DM)

> "Hi, is this [Owner]? Hey [Name], my name's [Your Name] — I'm a local web
> designer here on the South Shore. Quick reason I'm calling: I noticed
> [Business Name] doesn't have a website and I actually put together a sample
> one for you. Can I text or email you the link to check out? … Great, what's
> the best number/email?"

---

## D. Follow-up (3–4 days later, no response)

> Hi [Name], just bumping this up — did you get a chance to look at the mockup
> for [Business Name]? [link] No worries if it's not the right time, just didn't
> want it to get buried. — [Your Name]

**Rule:** follow up twice, then leave them alone. Mark SKIP and move on.

---

## Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We're fine with Facebook." | "Totally — this works *with* your Facebook, but it shows up on Google when someone searches '[their service] near me,' which FB doesn't. That's customers you're missing right now." |
| "How much?" | "One-time build is [$X], live on your own domain. Optional [$Y/mo] if you want me to host it and make changes for you. No long contract." |
| "I don't have time for this." | "That's the best part — you don't do anything. Send me your logo and hours, I handle the rest. Live in a few days." |
| "I'll think about it." | "Sounds good. The mockup's yours to look at — I'll check back Friday. If you've got a logo handy, text it over and I'll drop it in so you see the real thing." |
| "Can my nephew just do it?" | "For sure he could! Difference is I'll have it done this week, it'll show up on Google, and you've got someone to call when you need a change. Worth the peace of mind for most folks." |
