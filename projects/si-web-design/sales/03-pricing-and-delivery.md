# Pricing & Delivery

## Pricing (Staten Island small-business rates)

Keep it simple — one-time build + optional monthly. Most local owners want
"set it and forget it."

| Package | One-time | Monthly | What's included |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $400 | — | 1-page site (what's in the demos), mobile-friendly, hooked to their domain. You hand it off, they own it. |
| **Care Plan** ⭐ | $400 | $40/mo | Starter + you host it, free small edits (hours, photos, prices), keep it online, basic support. **Pitch this one — recurring income.** |
| **Pro** | $750 | $50/mo | Multi-section site, contact/quote form → their email, Google Business setup, basic SEO, gallery. |
| **Add-ons** | | | Logo design $100 · extra pages $75 ea · monthly Google posts $75/mo |

**Why the Care Plan is the play:** 15 clients × $40/mo = **$600/mo recurring**
on top of build fees, for ~an hour of edits a month. That's the actual business.

**Discount lever:** "First 5 Staten Island businesses get the build for $250."
Creates urgency and gets you reviews/portfolio fast.

## Delivery checklist (per client)

1. **Collect** (5-min text or visit): logo, 3–6 photos, hours, services, phone,
   address, a sentence about the business.
2. **Build:** copy `template/`, rename folder, Find & Replace all `[[PLACEHOLDERS]]`,
   set the 2 brand colors, drop in photos. ~30–60 min once you've done a few.
3. **Domain:**
   - They own one already → get login, point it (or send them DNS records).
   - They don't → buy at Namecheap/Porkbun (~$12/yr), bill it through. Use
     `businessname.com` or `.nyc`.
4. **Host (free):** drag the folder to **app.netlify.com/drop** → instant live
   link. Connect their domain in Netlify (free SSL included). Or GitHub Pages.
5. **Email form (Pro):** swap the demo form to **Formspree** (free tier) so
   submissions go to their inbox — no server needed.
6. **Google Business Profile:** claim/optimize it (free) — this is what actually
   gets them found. Huge value-add, costs you 15 minutes.
7. **Handoff:** send them the live link + a 2-line "how to request changes" note.
8. **Ask for a review** of *your* service once they're happy. Reviews = next clients.

## Tools you'll use (all free or near-free)

- **Hosting:** Netlify Drop (free) or GitHub Pages (free)
- **Domains:** Namecheap / Porkbun (~$12/yr)
- **Forms:** Formspree free tier
- **Photos (if they have none):** Unsplash / Pexels (free, commercial-ok) — the
  demos already pull from Unsplash
- **Logo (quick):** Canva free
- **Invoicing/payment:** Stripe Payment Links, Square, or Zelle/Venmo for locals

## Realistic ramp

- **Week 1:** build list (20–30 leads), make 3–4 mockups from the demos.
- **Week 2:** outreach — walk-ins + DMs. Goal: land 1–2 paying clients.
- **Month 1:** 3–5 clients. ~$1,200–2,000 in builds + start of recurring.
- **Month 3:** 10–15 clients, $400–600/mo recurring + referrals doing the
  prospecting for you.

Word-of-mouth on Staten Island is everything. Do 3 great jobs and the next 10
come to you.
