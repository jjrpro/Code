About the Local

Skilled. Certified. Union Proud.

Local 638 members are journeymen and apprentice steamfitters, welders, refrigeration mechanics, and HVAC technicians working under the United Association of Plumbers and Pipefitters. We've been organized since 1888, and every member completes a five-year, state-certified apprenticeship before earning journeyman status.

  • Five-year, no-cost apprenticeship with paid on-the-job training
  • OSHA-30, ASME B31, and AWS-certified welding curriculum
  • Family medical, dental, vision, and pension benefits from day one
  • Industry-leading wage scale negotiated under a master collective bargaining agreement
  • Lifelong access to advanced training: medical gas, HDPE fusion, BIM, and more

What We Do

Five Crafts. One Union.

Local 638 jurisdiction covers the full scope of high-pressure and process piping work in commercial, institutional, and industrial construction across the five boroughs and Long Island.

⚙

Steamfitting

High-pressure steam, condensate, hot and chilled water, fuel oil, and process piping systems in hospitals, universities, and high-rise construction.

🔥

Welding

Certified TIG, MIG, and stick welding to ASME Section IX, AWS D1.1, and B31 standards. In-house weld testing facility on premises.

Refrigeration & HVAC

Commercial refrigeration installation, controls, chillers, and process cooling. EPA 608 certified across all categories.

Medical & Specialty Gas

Apprenticeship

Become a Steamfitter. Earn While You Learn.

The Enterprise Association Steamfitters Local 638 JATC runs one of the most rigorous, well-respected apprenticeship programs in the trades. Five years. Paid from day one. No tuition. Full benefits. Lifetime career.

  • Starting wage at 50% of journeyman scale, raises every six months
  • 10,000 hours of paid on-the-job training
  • 900+ hours of classroom instruction at our LIC training center
  • State of New York & U.S. Department of Labor certified
  • Veterans receive preference under our Helmets to Hardhats partnership

For Contractors

Hire the Best-Trained Workforce in New York.

Signatory contractors get on-demand access to a roster of over 7,500 fully-vetted, code-certified mechanical tradespeople. From skyscraper construction to hospital service calls, Local 638 delivers safety, productivity, and quality you can underwrite.

  • Same-day dispatch through our business office
  • Predictable labor cost under master CBA — no scrambling for talent
  • OSHA-compliant, certified, drug-tested workforce
