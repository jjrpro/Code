## The brutal baseline

Most brand-new digital stores selling cold (no existing audience, no ad budget)
make **$0–$50 in month 1**, and a real share make **nothing**. That is the
median, not the worst case. Expect it.

## The math

- Only hard cost: **$49/mo** credit subscription.
- **Break-even = ~3–5 sales/month** (packs at $9–$19).
- Cold traffic converts ~**1–2%** → need ~100–200 visitors per 1–3 sales.
  Getting those visitors is the entire job.

## Scenarios

| Scenario | What it takes | Monthly revenue | Net after $49 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flop (most likely month 1) | Store up, little promo | $0–$30 | −$49 to −$19 |
| Modest (consistent posting 1–3 mo) | Daily organic posts | $80–$250 | +$30 to +$200 |
| Good (uncommon) | A post/pack catches, repeat buyers | $400–$900 | +$350 to +$850 |
| Big (rare) | Real audience built | $1k+ | varies |

## Honest read

- **Expected value is low and uncertain.** Lottery-ticket-with-effort, not a salary.
- **Real investment = the posting, not the $49.** Stores that work have someone
  showing up daily for weeks. Claude writes the posts; JR must actually post them;
  the algorithm decides.
- **Why it can still be worth it:** downside capped at ~$49/mo (cancel anytime),
  product built once sells forever, ~98% margin (just files). Asymmetric bet —
  small capped loss, uncapped-ish upside.

## Recommendation

Treat it as a **cheap 30-day experiment**, not an income plan. Fund one month,
Claude builds + promotes, JR posts daily. If still near $0 after ~30 days of real
posting, kill it — out $49 and a lesson, nothing more.
