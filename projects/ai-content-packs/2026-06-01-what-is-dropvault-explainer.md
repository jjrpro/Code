## What DropVault is (simple version)

**An online store that sells digital "content packs" — you never touch the
product or ship anything.**

- **The product:** bundles of ready-made AI media — e.g. "100 phone wallpapers"
  or "60 cinematic video clips." Just files.
- **Who buys it:** people running TikTok / Instagram / YouTube pages who
  constantly need fresh visuals and would rather pay ~$9–$49 for a ready pack
  than make their own.
- **How a sale works:** visitor opens the store → taps Buy → pays by card →
  store **automatically** sends the download link. No human in the loop.
- **Why it's good:** make a pack **once**, it can sell **forever**. No inventory,
  no shipping, no support. Pure digital.
- **Claude's job:** create the packs (with AI credits), stock the store, write
  the promo, report sales each session.
- **JR's job:** fund credits once, set up one payout account, approve things.

**The honest catch:** a brand-new store has no visitors. Selling isn't automatic
— we have to drive eyeballs to it (free, but takes posting). That's the real
work, and Claude does most of it.
