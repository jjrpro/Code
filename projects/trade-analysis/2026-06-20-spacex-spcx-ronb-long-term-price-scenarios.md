**Date:** 2026-06-20
**Summary:** Scenario-based long-term price ranges for SPCX and RONB, built from

## Caveat
Algorithmic "forecast" sites produce unreliable numbers (one showed SPCX at
$11.28 for 2027 — never adjusted for the real $135 IPO). Ignored. Anchors used:
Wall Street analyst targets + Ark Invest 2030 valuation model + Morgan Stanley.

## SPCX (SpaceX stock)

**12-month analyst targets:** avg **~$188–235**, high **~$310**, low **~$62**
(S&P Global / TipRanks / Investing.com). The 5x low-to-high spread reflects huge
disagreement (no trading history to anchor on).

**Long-term (Ark Invest 2030 valuation → implied share price, share count flat):**

| Scenario | 2030 valuation | ≈ Share price | vs. ~$185 today |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bear | $1.7T | ~$135 | −27% (below $165 entry) |
| Base | $2.5T | ~$200 | +8% |
| Bull | $3.1T | ~$250 | +35% |
| Hyper-bull ($4T+) | $4T+ | ~$320+ | +73%+ |

**Key insight:** IPO'd at ~$2.3T — already inside Ark's 2030 range. Even the
base case is only ~8% above today over 5 years. The IPO price already bakes in
heavy growth; real upside requires the BULL scenarios (Starship at scale,
Starlink ~$300B revenue by 2035). Morgan Stanley models $3.4T *revenue* by 2040
(a 2040 story). Dilution from secondary offerings would lower implied prices.

## RONB (ETF)
No published price targets (young active fund). Price tracks its basket (~21%

| Scenario | ≈ RONB price |
|---|---|
| Bear | ~$22–24 (flat/down) |
| Base | ~$30–34 |
| Bull | ~$38–42 |

Diversified — won't moonshot with SpaceX or crash with it. 1% fee eats ~5%+ of
returns over 5 years.

## Honest take
Don't anchor to a single target — the spread is too wide. Base case = modest
(already priced rich); real upside only if Starship/Starlink deliver; real
downside if they stumble. Use the trigger-based exit plan
(`2026-06-20-spacex-spcx-ronb-exit-plan.md`) rather than betting on a price.
