## SpaceX (SPCX) — what outlets are saying

- **Largest IPO in history.** IPO'd **June 12, 2026** on Nasdaq. Priced at
  **$135**, opened $150, closed day one at **$160.95 (+19%)**, valued **above
  $2 trillion** — beating Saudi Aramco's record. (CNBC, NPR, CNN, Yahoo Finance)
- **Analysts lean bullish:** ~6 buy / 1 sell → overall **"Buy."** (Investing.com)
- **Fundamentals cited:** ~$16B revenue & $8B EBITDA (53% margin) in 2025;
  **Starlink** = $11.4B revenue (+48% YoY), 10M+ subscribers across 160 markets;
  2026 projected $27–30B revenue, ~$5B FCF. (Sacra, intellectia, spacexstock)
- **Watch-outs:** rich valuation ($1.75–2T); post-IPO **insider lockup / quiet
  period** is the near-term overhang. **Starship reaching operational status**
  is the next big catalyst.

## Key takeaway

**SpaceX is now public — RONB's original selling point (pre-IPO access) is gone.**
The IPO pop sent SPCX **+19%** but moved RONB only **~+3%** — the basket effect
diluting both upside and single-stock risk. From here RONB is simply an actively
managed fund holding ~21% SpaceX for a 1% annual fee. Fine to hold for
diversification; just no longer paying for early access.
