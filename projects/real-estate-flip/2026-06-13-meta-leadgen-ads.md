# Meta Lead-Gen Ads — Cash-Buyer List Build

**Date:** June 13, 2026
**Summary:** Paste-ready Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ad campaign to build a NJ/NY
cash-buyer list for the wholesaling business. Targeting, copy variants, and
setup that match JR's existing ad experience from JaurxShops.

---

## Campaign setup

- **Objective:** Leads (use an Instant Form — cheaper + higher completion than
  sending to the landing page; landing page is the backup/retargeting).
- **Budget:** start $10-15/day. Kill/scale at the 50-lead mark.
- **Placements:** Advantage+ (let Meta optimize), but exclude Audience Network.
- **Geo:** 25-mi radius around Jersey City + Newark + Staten Island (covers
  Hudson, Essex, Union, Richmond, parts of Bergen/Middlesex).
- **Age:** 30-65+.

## Targeting (detailed)

Interests / behaviors (broad is fine — Advantage+ will refine):
- Real estate investing · Real estate investment trust · Flipping (real estate)
- BiggerPockets · DealMachine · PropStream
- Landlord · Buy and hold · Rental property
- Behaviors: Small business owners; "likely to move/engaged investors"

Lookalike (once you have 50+ leads): 1% lookalike of your buyer-list uploads.

## Instant Form fields (keep it short)
1. Full name (auto)
2. Email (auto)
3. Phone (auto)
4. "Which counties do you buy in?" (short answer)
5. "Max purchase price?" (multiple choice: <$150k / $150-350k / $350-600k / $600k+)
6. "How do you buy?" (Cash / Hard money / Cash + financing)

Completion message: "You're in. We'll text you deals that fit your buy box.
Reply STOP to opt out anytime."

## Creative direction (use your JaurxShops ad skills)
- Simple, high-contrast: a NJ/NY rowhouse/2-family photo + bold text overlay
  "OFF-MARKET DEALS — CASH BUYERS LIST."
- Or a short selfie-style Reel: "If you buy investment property in NJ or NY,
  watch this" → 15s pitch → "link to join below."
- Avoid stocky luxury mansions — show realistic 2-4 unit / rowhouse stock that
  matches actual deal flow.

## Compliance notes
- You're advertising a BUYER list, not soliciting distressed sellers — this
  side has no HETPA/foreclosure-consultant exposure. Clean.
- Honor STOP/opt-out (TCPA) on any texts you send to collected numbers.
- Don't imply you're a licensed broker. You're an investor offering assignable
  contracts.

## After launch
- Export leads daily into `cash-buyer-crm.csv`.
- Once at 50+ leads, build a 1% lookalike and scale budget.
- Retarget landing-page visitors who didn't submit.
