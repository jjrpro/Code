# JR's Wholesaling Launch Plan — 2-3 hrs/week, Buyer-List-First

**Date:** June 13, 2026
**Summary:** 30-day plan to get JR's NY/NJ wholesaling business producing,
built around his real constraint (2-3 hrs/week) and his real edge (audience
building + paid ads). Model: build a cash-buyer list first, co-wholesale other
people's contracts for split fees, then layer in automated acquisition later.

---

## The honest setup

- **Constraint:** 2-3 hrs/week. Cannot run hands-on acquisition (driving for
  dollars / cold calling needs 10-20+ hrs). Plan must respect this.
- **Edge:** JR builds audiences (JaurxTrades Telegram) and runs Meta/TikTok ads
  (JaurxShops). That's the disposition (buyer-side) skill most wholesalers lack.
- **Chosen model:** DISPO / CO-WHOLESALE. Build a NJ/NY cash-buyer list; bring
  buyers to active wholesalers' contracts; split the assignment fee (typically
  50/50). Low time, plays to his strength, learns the business on someone else's
  acquisition risk. Layer paid-ad acquisition later once a deal pays.

---

## Phase 0 — Foundation (Week 1, ~2 hrs)

1. **LLC + EIN + business bank account.** NJ or NY LLC (~$125 NJ / $200 NY).
   EIN free at irs.gov. Cheap, do it first.
2. **One-time attorney consult** — NJ/NY real estate attorney. Have them give
   you: (a) a purchase agreement WITH an assignment clause, (b) an assignment
   agreement, (c) a JV/co-wholesale agreement template. This is the expense not
   to skip. ~$300-500 one-time.
3. **Google Voice number + simple landing page** (you build sites — 1 hour).
   "We buy houses cash, NJ/NY" + a "cash buyers — get our deal alerts" opt-in.

## Phase 1 — Build the cash-buyer list (Weeks 1-3, your edge)

Goal: 25-50 active NJ/NY cash buyers you can blast a deal to. Sources:

1. **Public cash-sale records** — recent all-cash purchases = active investors
   (county recorder / ACRIS: deeds with no mortgage). Skip trace the buyer.
2. **Local REIA meetings** — NJ/NY real estate investor associations. Cash
   buyers are literally there to find deals. Collect contacts.
3. **Facebook groups** — "NJ Real Estate Investors", "NYC Wholesale Deals", etc.
   Post that you source deals; collect buyer criteria.
4. **Your ad skill** — a tiny Meta lead-gen campaign targeting "real estate
   investor" interests → "Get off-market NJ/NY deals" opt-in. You already know
   this playbook from Shopify.
5. **BiggerPockets** marketplace + forums — find buyers by market.

Capture each buyer's: name, phone/email, target counties, property type, max
price, cash/financing, buy box. Store in a simple sheet.

## Phase 2 — Source deals to assign (Weeks 2-4, low-time)

You are NOT grinding acquisition. You are:

1. **Co-wholesaling** — connect with 5-10 active NJ/NY wholesalers (same FB
   groups / REIA). Tell them: "I have cash buyers, send me your deals, we split."
   You move their contract to your buyer, split the fee. Zero acquisition time.
2. **On-market stale/as-is** — your buyer's agent or Zillow filter (60+ DOM,
   "as-is", price cuts). These CAN sometimes be assigned/double-closed. Low
   effort, you already have the screening worksheet.
3. **Later (when a deal pays):** turn on automated acquisition — PPC or
   batch-texting via a service/VA so leads come without your hours.

## Phase 3 — Close + collect (ongoing)

1. Match a sourced deal to a buyer in your list (buy box + MAO math).
2. Lock contract (or co-wholesale JV with the wholesaler who has it).
3. Assign to your buyer; collect assignment fee at closing (attorney/title).
4. Reinvest first fee into paid acquisition to scale.

---

## Deal math (same as the screening worksheet)
`MAO = (ARV x 0.70) - repairs - your assignment fee`
For co-wholesale, your cut = (agreed split) of the total assignment fee.

## Legal guardrails (don't skip)
- Foreclosure-stage seller outreach = NY HETPA / NJ Foreclosure Rescue Fraud
  Prevention Act. Co-wholesaling on-market or non-foreclosure deals sidesteps
  most of that.
- Always disclose you're assigning. You sell YOUR CONTRACT, not the property.
- NY/NJ are attorney-closing states — use one every deal.
