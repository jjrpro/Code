# Co-Wholesale Outreach Scripts + Buyer Buy-Box Intake

**Date:** June 13, 2026
**Summary:** Paste-ready scripts to (1) open co-wholesale relationships with
active NJ/NY wholesalers who have contracts but need buyers, and (2) intake a
cash buyer's "buy box" so you only send them matching deals. This is the
low-time engine of JR's dispo-first model.

---

## PART 1 — Co-wholesale outreach (to other wholesalers)

Where to find them: NJ/NY REIA meetups, Facebook groups ("New Jersey Real
Estate Investors", "NYC / NJ Wholesale Real Estate Deals"), BiggerPockets
marketplace, Instagram (#njwholesale #njrealestate).

### FB group post (cast a wide net)
> NJ/NY wholesalers — I run a growing cash-buyer list (active flippers +
> landlords across Hudson, Essex, Union, Richmond + more). If you've got
> contracts you're struggling to move, send them my way — I'll bring a buyer
> and we split the assignment fee 50/50. DM me. Quick closers only. 🤝

### Cold DM (to a specific wholesaler)
> Hey [name] — saw your deal in [group/post]. I keep an active NJ/NY cash-buyer
> list and move contracts fast. If you ever have a deal that's sitting, I'll
> JV with you — I bring the buyer, we split the fee 50/50. Want me to take a
> look at what you've got under contract right now?

### When they send a deal — your reply
> Got it. Send me: address, contract price, your assignment fee, est. repairs,
> ARV/comps, and the deadline. I'll run it past my buyers today and tell you
> straight if I can move it. We'll paper a quick JV agreement before I share it.

### The rule
Always sign a **co-wholesale / JV agreement** BEFORE you market their deal or
introduce your buyer (protects your fee — stops getting cut out). Use the
attorney template from the launch plan.

## PART 2 — Cash-buyer buy-box intake

When a buyer joins (form, ad, or REIA), capture this so you can match deals
fast. Log into `cash-buyer-crm.csv`.

### Intake questions (text or call)
1. Name + best phone + email
2. **Counties/towns** you buy in
3. **Property type** (SFR / 2-4 unit / multifamily / land / any)
4. **Max purchase price** and ideal price band
5. **Condition** you'll take (light cosmetic / full gut / any)
6. **How you fund** (cash / hard money / cash+financing) — proof of funds?
7. **How fast** you can close (days)
8. **Target return** (min spread / cap rate / ARV ratio)
9. **Volume** — how many deals/month do you want?
10. Best way + time to reach you when a deal drops

### Buyer text after intake (sets expectations)
> You're on the list 🤝 I'll only send deals that fit your box: [counties],
> [type], up to [price], [condition]. When one drops I'll text address +
> numbers — if it fits, move fast, deals go quick. Reply STOP to opt out.

### Deal-blast template (when you have a property to move)
> 🏠 NEW DEAL — [town], [county]
> [beds]bd/[baths]ba [type], [sqft] sqft
> Price: $[contract+fee]  |  Est. repairs: $[x]  |  ARV: $[y]
> Spread/margin: ~$[z]
> Cash/quick close. First to commit gets it. Want the full packet? Reply YES.

## Compliance reminders
- Co-wholesaling on on-market or non-foreclosure deals avoids HETPA / NJ
  Foreclosure Rescue Fraud Prevention Act exposure.
- You sell your CONTRACT / your buyer relationship — not the property. Don't
  market a property you have no equitable interest in (that's unlicensed
  brokering). JV agreement = your equitable interest.
- Honor STOP/opt-out on all texts (TCPA).
- Use a title company/attorney every close.
