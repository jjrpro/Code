## Strategy + legal notes (READ FIRST)

- **These are sheriff-sale auctions, not MLS listings.** Contract-assignment
  wholesaling does NOT work at the auction itself — you bid, win, pay ~20%
  deposit on the spot, balance in ~30 days. No assignment clause.
- **To wholesale these, target the owner BEFORE the sale date** (pre-foreclosure).
  That triggers NJ's **Foreclosure Rescue Fraud Prevention Act**: mandatory
  written disclosures, cancellation rights, criminal penalties. Use an attorney.
- **No debt/judgment amounts in the feed.** Pull each property's judgment +
  open liens from the county before running deal math. Junior liens can survive;
  a property can sell for less than what's owed.
- **NJ redemption window:** owners can sometimes redeem post-sale. Factor into
  exit timing.
- **Data not warranted accurate** by the county — verify every entry on the
  source portal before acting.
