## The honest math that shapes this
- Buyer leads (Meta lead-gen): ~$3-8 each. CHEAP. Build a buyer list fast.
- Seller leads ("we buy houses"): ~$50-150 each. EXPENSIVE. A $300-800 budget
  buys only a handful — enough to SEED a pipeline, not reliably close in 30 days.
- Therefore: the Day-30 check comes from **co-wholesale** (bring a buyer to a
  deal another wholesaler already has under contract; split the fee). Own
  sourced deals = month 2 payoff.

## TRACK A — Co-wholesale (the Day-30 check)

**Goal:** one split-fee assignment by day 30.

- Ads build the buyer list (set-and-watch; JR knows Meta).
- JR's hours go to: (1) intake buyers into the CRM, (2) open relationships with
  active NJ/NY wholesalers, (3) match one of their deals to a buyer, (4) sign a

## TRACK B — Own seller deals (month-2 payoff, seeded now)

- Small seller ad spend → seller leads land in the seller-site form.
- JR (or a cheap VA later) calls them back, runs numbers, makes an offer.
- Anything that contracts gets assigned to the buyer list built in Track A.
- Don't expect this to close in 30 days; it's the pipeline that pays month 2+.

## Prerequisites that gate getting PAID (do in week 1)
- LLC + EIN + business bank account (so the title company can pay your entity).
- Attorney JV + assignment templates (so your fee is protected and legal).
- These are non-negotiable before a check changes hands.

## Success metric
- **Day 30:** 1 co-wholesale assignment fee collected (even $2-5k is a win + proof).
- 40-80 cash buyers on the list (the durable asset — pays every future deal).
- 2-5 seller leads in the pipeline for month 2.

## Honest risk flags
- 30 days is tight; the variable you don't control is whether a wholesaler
  contact hands you a movable deal in time. The buyer list is the asset that
  makes EVERY future month faster — even if the first check slips a week or two.
- Foreclosure-stage seller leads (Track B) trigger NY HETPA / NJ Foreclosure
  Rescue Fraud Prevention Act — use the attorney disclosures. Co-wholesale
  on-market/non-foreclosure deals avoids most of that.
