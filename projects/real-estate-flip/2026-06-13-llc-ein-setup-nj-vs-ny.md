# LLC + EIN Setup — NJ vs NY for Wholesaling

**Date:** June 13, 2026
**Summary:** Step-by-step to stand up the legal entity JR needs before he can
contract, bank, or collect an assignment fee. Includes the NJ-vs-NY decision,
exact filing steps, costs, and what to do right after.

---

## Decision: form the LLC in NEW JERSEY

Why NJ over NY for your wholesaling LLC:
- **NY has the "publication requirement"** — new LLCs must publish formation
  notice in two newspapers for 6 weeks, often **$300-$1,500+** (NYC counties are
  the most expensive in the state). Pure cost with no benefit to you.
- **NJ has no publication requirement.** Filing is **$125**, done online, same day.
- Most of your early deal flow (Hudson/Essex/Newark/Jersey City/Bayonne) is in
  NJ anyway.
- You can still wholesale NY (Staten Island) deals as an NJ LLC — if NY volume
  grows later, register as a "foreign LLC" in NY then (and handle publication
  at that point).

> Not legal/tax advice — confirm with your attorney/CPA, especially if most
> income will actually be NY-sourced.

## Step 1 — Form the NJ LLC (~30 min, $125)

1. Go to **nj.gov/treasury/revenue** → "Business Registration" →
   "Register a New Business" (the NJ Business Formation portal).
2. Entity type: **Domestic Limited Liability Company (LLC)**.
3. Business name: pick one that's available (NJ name search is in the portal).
   Avoid "realty/brokerage" words that imply you're a licensed broker.
4. Registered agent: you can be your own (use your address) or pay a service
   (~$100-150/yr) to keep your home address off public record.
5. Pay the **$125** filing fee. You get a **Certificate of Formation**.

## Step 2 — File the NJ Business Registration (NJ-REG)

NJ requires a **NJ-REG** within 60 days of formation (same Treasury portal).
This registers you for tax purposes. Free.

## Step 3 — Get the EIN (free, 10 min, IRS)

1. **irs.gov** → search "Apply for an EIN online" (Mon-Fri, business hours).
2. Entity = LLC; member count = however many owners.
3. EIN issued instantly. **Do NOT pay a third-party site** — the IRS is free.

## Step 4 — Operating Agreement

- Even a single-member LLC should have one. Your attorney can supply a template
  with the formation docs; this is part of the one-time consult in the launch
  plan.

## Step 5 — Business bank account

Bring to the bank (or open online): Certificate of Formation, EIN letter,
Operating Agreement, ID. Keep business money separate from personal — this is
what preserves the LLC's liability protection. Good fee-free options: local
credit union, or online (Bluevine, Mercury, Found, Relay).

## Step 6 — Couple of basics

- **Business address:** a virtual mailbox or your home; keep it consistent
  across LLC, EIN, bank, and website.
- **Phone:** Google Voice number (free) for the business line.
- **Bookkeeping:** simplest = a dedicated bank account + a spreadsheet now;
  Wave (free) or QuickBooks later.
