**Purpose**: Eliminate the "what do I post today?" decision. Each day below has the exact text or fill-in-the-blank template to paste into TikTok, X, and your free JaurxTrades Telegram.

**How to use**:
1. Open this file on your Mac (Obsidian: `projects/jaurx-vip/launch/2026-05-31-30-day-content-pack.md`)
2. Scroll to today's date
3. Fill in any `[BRACKETED]` blanks with today's actual numbers (price, R, P&L)
4. Copy → paste into the platform → post

## What this pack does NOT include (and why)

- **Specific levels per day** — these depend on what the market actually does. You fill them in each morning based on your usual pre-market routine. (Takes 5 min.)
- **Specific P&L numbers** — these are your real trades. The template format works whether you made $20 or $2000.
- **Video editing instructions** — TikTok's built-in editor + a clear voiceover is enough. Don't optimize. Optimize quantity first.
- **Hashtag spam** — algos in 2026 punish keyword-stuffing. Use 3–5 specific tags: #scalping #futurestrading #mgc #mnq #daytrader. Done.
