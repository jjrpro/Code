# Cross-Promo Target List — Trader Telegrams to DM for Shoutout Swaps

**Purpose**: List of trader Telegram channels in the 500–5000 member range (the sweet spot for shoutout swaps — big enough to add real subs, small enough that owners actually respond to DMs).

**How to use**:
1. Verify each channel is still active (last post < 7 days) by clicking the link
2. Look up the owner's handle from the channel's "Owner" or pinned post
3. Send the DM template (below) — one per day across 10–14 days
4. Track responses in `2026-06-cross-promo-tracker.md` (Claude will create when first reply comes in)

**Realistic conversion**:
- 20 DMs sent → ~5–7 replies → ~3–4 actual swaps booked → ~150–400 new free subs total

## Target categories (what to look for)

**A-tier swaps (DM these first)**:
- US futures-focused (MGC, MNQ, MES, ES, NQ scalpers and intraday traders)
- Member count: 500–3000
- Real human owner (not bot-run signals channel)
- Posts visible to non-members (helps you verify content quality)
- "Real trades" or "live calls" in description (matches your positioning)

**B-tier swaps (after A-tier responds)**:
- Broader day-trading channels (includes options + futures mixed)
- 1000–5000 members
- Active daily

**Avoid (these waste DMs)**:
- Anything with "signals" or "guaranteed" in the name
- Crypto/forex channels (different audience, low conversion)
- Channels under 200 members (your share would be bigger than theirs)
- Pure copy-trade or prop-firm shilling channels

## Where to find target channels (4 sources, no money)

### Source 1: Telegram Discovery
Open Telegram → search bar → type "futures trading" / "scalping" / "MGC trading" / "MNQ trading" / "day trading futures". Sort by member count. Note any channel 500–5000 that posts daily.

### Source 2: telemetr.io (free directory)
URL: `https://telemetr.io/en/channels/categories/business-trading`
Filter to English, sort by recent activity. Pull 30 candidates. Check each for the A-tier criteria above.

### Source 3: Twitter trader bios
Scroll your Twitter timeline for trader accounts with a Telegram link in bio. They WILL have a channel. Many run small-to-mid Telegram groups perfect for swaps.

### Source 4: r/Daytrading + r/Futures mod sidebar
Some active subreddit users link their Telegram in their profile or comment signatures. Cross-reference.

## What you give to the swap (the post YOU post about them)

Standard format you can reuse for every swap:

**For your Telegram**:
```
🔁 Shoutout to a channel I respect — [@CHANNEL]

[Owner name] posts [WHAT THEY POST: e.g., "clean ES setups with full thesis"] —
been following for a while. If you trade [INSTRUMENT/STYLE], check it out:

[LINK]

I rarely shout other channels out. This one's worth your time.
```

**For your Twitter (same day)**:
```
Following @[their twitter] for [WHAT]. Clean trader, no hype. Worth a follow
if you scalp [INSTRUMENT]: [link to their channel]
```

The "I rarely shout other channels out" line is critical — it preserves your channel's signal-to-noise ratio. Don't do more than 1 swap-shoutout per week or your own audience tunes out.

## What you should expect FROM them (the post they post about you)

When negotiating, send them YOUR preferred post copy so they don't write generic filler. Paste-ready for them:

the moment he takes them. Tradovate-specific community.

If they push back on the wording, fine — but having a draft prevents lazy 2-line posts that don't convert.
