## The honest framing

Traders don't subscribe to anonymous Telegram links. They subscribe to **people they've seen win AND lose** over enough time to believe they have an edge. So the entire 30-day plan boils down to one thing: **be visible, consistent, and transparent across 2–3 channels where traders hang out, with content that proves edge.** Subscribers follow.

Three platforms matter for free traders' traction in 2026: **TikTok**, **Twitter/X**, and **Telegram cross-promotion**. Everything else is noise at this stage.

## The 3 highest-leverage moves (in priority order)

### MOVE 1 — TikTok organic, daily during US session

**Why this is #1**: TikTok's algorithm in 2026 still pushes trading content hard, especially anything under 90 seconds with charts + voiceover. Cost: zero. Reach: thousands per post if you nail it.

**Daily routine (~30 min/day)**:
1. Pick ONE chart from your live trading day — entry, fill, exit, P&L
2. Record screen of the chart (Tradovate or TradingView) with your voiceover explaining: "Here's the setup I saw. Here's why I took it. Here's what happened."
3. Add Telegram link sticker + "FREE alerts in bio" overlay
4. Post within 2 hours of the trade closing

**Content formats that work for traders (in order of viral potential)**:
1. **"I lost $X today — here's why" loss breakdowns** ← highest trust-build
2. **"This setup paid me $X in 4 minutes"** wins (only 1 in 4 — too many = scammy)
3. **"3 mistakes scalpers make on MGC"** educational
4. **Macro reactions** ("CPI just printed, here's what I see at the open")
5. **Tradovate platform tips** (clean traffic from non-buyers who become buyers later)

**Hooks that stop the scroll** (first 1.5 sec):
- "I just blew up my account doing this"
- "I made $400 in 3 minutes. Here's exactly how."
- "If you don't know this, the prop firms will eat you alive"
- "Stop trading MGC like this"
- "This is why scalpers lose money on Tuesdays"

**Bio**: clean handle (@JaurxTrades), 1-line value prop ("Live MGC + MNQ scalp alerts — free Telegram"), single link to Telegram via Linktree or direct.

**Goal**: 20+ posts in first 30 days. At least 1 will hit (10k+ views). That one drives 50–200 subs.

**Process (one weekend afternoon)**:

**Who to look for**:

## Anti-patterns (avoid these)

1. **Posting trades AFTER they happen with no real-time proof** — the entire scammer playbook. Telegram timestamps your posts; use that. Always post entry BEFORE you know the outcome.
2. **Buying followers** — kills your algo reach. Algos detect engagement-to-follower ratios. 1000 fake followers = lower reach than 200 real ones.
3. **Asking for follows in every post** — works once, then audience tunes out. Make 80% of posts pure value, 20% ask.
4. **Selling VIP too early in someone's content journey** — let them see you trade 5–10 times first. Trust compounds; CTAs don't.
5. **Reposting same content across all 3 platforms verbatim** — algos detect cross-posts and bury them. Rewrite the hook for each platform.
