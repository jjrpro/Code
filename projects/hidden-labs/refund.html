Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

We want you to receive exactly what you ordered, in good condition. Because of the nature of research compounds, this policy balances customer protection with product-integrity and safety requirements.

Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact us within 7 days of delivery at admin@jjrproconsultants.com with your order number and clear photos. We will make it right with a free replacement or a refund.

Lost or non-delivered orders

If tracking shows your order did not arrive, contact us within 14 days of the expected delivery date and we will investigate with the carrier and replace or refund eligible orders.

Returns of opened products

For product-integrity and contamination-control reasons, opened vials cannot be accepted for return. Unopened items in their original, unused condition may be eligible for return at our discretion if requested within 14 days of delivery; return shipping is the buyer's responsibility unless the return is due to our error.

Order changes & cancellations

Need to change or cancel? Email us as soon as possible. We can usually amend an order before it is dispatched; once shipped, an order cannot be cancelled but may fall under the sections above.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. For crypto payments, refunds are sent to the wallet you provide (refund value is based on the U.S.-dollar order amount). Please allow several business days for processing.

Contact

Start any refund or return request at admin@jjrproconsultants.com.
